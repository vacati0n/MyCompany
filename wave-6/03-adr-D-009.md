# Architecture Decision Record D-009

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-009
- Title: A deterministic copyright-check handler and a rights-check step in the rule-determined assembly are the recorder's first production caller
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-7; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0007; planner tasks T-008, T-020 and T-034; sequencing constraint P-011

## Context

- Problem statement: the four record sources are written only by the dossier recorder, and the recorder has no production caller because no stage handler exists, so in the company's own store every source reads unmeasured; where the first handler lives is this role's to decide.
- Business and technical constraints: C-003 forbids any spend, so the caller must perform no metered operation; C-005 keeps deterministic work incapable of a model call and in the rule-determined assembly; C-001 forbids any position after composition; C-011 forbids re-creating the recorder.
- Current architecture baseline: F-023 records the recorder, the handler contract and the absence of any handler; F-024 records the copyright-check stage and the zero-cost rights rules already named; F-025 records the write-once dossier tables and the recorded channel; F-021 records the rights precondition; F-031 records the rule-determined assembly's references.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: a copyright-check handler implements the delivered stage handler contract in the rule-determined assembly; it reads the item's recorded assets and its recorded channel's registrations and returns stage evidence of the delivered rights precondition's verdict, performing no metered operation. A rights-check step in the same assembly carries a new item version: it opens the dossier through the delivered recorder where none is opened, submits the item through the gate service, runs the handler, records its evidence through the recorder, then presents the item where the verdict is releasable or sends it back where it is not. The item's channel is read from the recorded dossier and never accepted from a caller. Each step reads recorded state first, so an interrupted step resumes. Nothing touches the publishing sequence.
- Scope of impact: M-018 is the handler; M-017 is the step; M-016 supplies the gate members; M-029, the recorder, is unchanged; M-023 places both types in the rule-determined assembly.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-008, a handler for a stage that reaches a capability, placed with the capability boundary.
- Benefits: a handler for a stage the master plan treats as central.
- Risks: it needs a metered operation and leaves the rule-determined assembly.
- Why not selected: it violates C-003 and C-005 and is eliminated.

2. Writing the dossier from the publishing entry point, considered within O-002.
- Benefits: no new step.
- Risks: it mixes the production path into the publishing sequence, which must add nothing.
- Why not selected: the rights check is a production stage with a deterministic rule of its own.

3. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007 and O-009, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: the dossier header and a stage outcome are written by a production code path through the recorder at zero metered operations, and the same step is the real-state path to owner approval.
- Tradeoffs accepted: the step spans several transactions and resumes from recorded state rather than committing atomically; an item with no recorded asset passes the delivered rights precondition.
- Risks introduced: R-017, the vacuous rights pass; R-018, an interrupted step.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of metered operations recorded by the step; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of record sources written by the step in a demonstration; the expected value is at least one, the dossier header and the copyright-check stage outcome.
  - The count of positions added to the publishing workflow; the expected value is zero, asserted on every solution build.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - The step driven against a throwaway store and the dossier header and stage outcome read back through the delivered reader.
  - The boundary assertions placing the handler and the step in the rule-determined assembly.
  - A step interrupted after its evidence write and resumed, ending with one evidence row and the expected gate state.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if a different first stage is preferred; removing the handler and the step leaves the recorder and the gate service unchanged.
