# Architecture Decision Record D-008

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-008
- Title: A submission writes awaiting rights check, the presentation is admitted only from it with a releasable rights check, and a datastore check holds every gate transition to the recorded from-state
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-7; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0007; planner tasks T-010, T-022 and T-036; sequencing constraints P-001, P-010 and P-012

## Context

- Problem statement: no delivered path brings a new item to awaiting owner approval, because nothing writes the awaiting-rights-check state; the presentation does not consult the table itself and relies on the writer to refuse a missing edge; and neither the writer nor its double compares the caller's claimed from-state with the recorded state, so a caller can claim a state the record does not hold.
- Business and technical constraints: C-014 requires every gate change to start from the recorded current state and pass the table, with no edge from Draft bypassing the rights check; C-006 keeps owner approval a table state absent from configuration; C-002 keeps the three conditions refusing; C-015 requires additive change.
- Current architecture baseline: F-018 records the transition table; F-019 records the presentation, the writer and the double; F-020 records that no production code writes awaiting rights check and how the delivered fixtures reach it; F-021 records the rights precondition and the caller-supplied channel of publish evaluation; F-022 records the roles holding approval presentation; F-035 records how the current state is read.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the gate service gains a submission member that brings an item version from its recorded state to awaiting rights check where the table admits that edge, which it admits from Draft only, under a role holding approval presentation, writing the transition and its entry on one transaction. The presentation reads the recorded state and refuses by name, writing only a refusal entry, unless the recorded state is awaiting rights check and the delivered rights precondition is releasable for the item's recorded channel; the writer's table check remains behind it. A check on the gate-transition record holds the item row exclusively for the writing transaction and refuses a transition whose from-state differs from the item version's latest recorded state, Draft where none is recorded. The table, the publish predicate and the three conditions are unchanged.
- Scope of impact: M-001 adds the check; M-016 gains the submission and the refusing presentation; M-024 mirrors the check in the in-memory double; M-025 moves the delivered fixtures to the submission; M-027 and M-032 are unchanged.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-009, admitting the presentation straight from Draft by adding an edge to the table, with no rights-check writer.
- Benefits: the smallest change to reach awaiting owner approval.
- Risks: an edge from Draft bypasses the rights check and the table changes.
- Why not selected: it violates C-014 and C-006 and is eliminated.

2. Comparing the claimed from-state in the writer and the double only, considered within O-002.
- Benefits: no datastore object.
- Risks: a writer bypassing the delivered writer could still claim a state the record does not hold, and two concurrent writers read the state outside their transactions.
- Why not selected: the datastore check binds every writer and serialises writes per item row.

3. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007 and O-008, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: a new item in Draft reaches awaiting owner approval on real recorded state through awaiting rights check; a presentation from any other state is refused by name; no caller can claim a state the record does not hold; the dispatch then reached is still refused by each undischarged condition.
- Tradeoffs accepted: delivered fixtures that wrote gate states directly move to the submission; one item row is held per gate write.
- Risks introduced: R-015, delivered callers refused; R-016, a gate write waiting on the item row; R-019, the caller-supplied channel of publish evaluation, raised separately.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of recorded transitions whose from-state differs from the state recorded before them; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of presentations recorded from a state other than awaiting rights check; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of production writers of the awaiting-rights-check state; the expected value is one.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - A throwaway store with no fixture gate row in which a new Draft item reaches awaiting owner approval, reading the audited transition sequence.
  - A presentation from Draft refused by name, and a transition claiming a state the record does not hold refused by the check.
  - The dispatch reached afterwards refused naming each undischarged condition with every condition at its production value.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the owner decides that presentation may skip the rights check, which would also be a change to the table; the service is restored and the check dropped independently.
