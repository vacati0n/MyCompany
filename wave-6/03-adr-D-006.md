# Architecture Decision Record D-006

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-006
- Title: Operations are stamped and booked by the datastore under the record horizon, and a month over the operation record reads final only once the horizon passes its end
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-7; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0007; planner tasks T-007, T-019 and T-033; sequencing constraints P-001, P-005 and P-007

## Context

- Problem statement: the previous change made an observed audit period final by construction, but the operation record carries an instant from the process clock, any writer may write any period, and no month reading over it — the cost readings, the tier ratio, their channel partitions and budget utilisation — is final; the risk was accepted and carried, with the route reserved to this role.
- Business and technical constraints: C-012 requires a month reading presented as final to be unchanged by any later operation, enforced in the datastore for every writer, under one rule for every month reading; C-009 keeps money arithmetic in the datastore; C-015 requires every existing row to keep its meaning; C-011 requires the delivered horizon to be extended, not re-created.
- Current architecture baseline: F-009 records the recorder writing the draft's process-clock instant and its period; F-010 records the capability boundary's transaction order and its budget evaluation period; F-012 records that every month reading selects by period; F-013 records the record horizon, its shared hold, its check and its non-waiting closure; F-038 records the row-hold behaviour it relies on; F-039 records the delivered checks recording at fixed instants.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the operation recorder stamps each operation's instant from the datastore as the later of its clock and the record horizon, under the same shared hold on the horizon the appender takes and keeps to the end of its transaction, resolves the price rows at that instant, books the operation into that instant's month, and returns the stored instant in the delivered instant member. A check on the operation record takes the same shared hold and refuses an operation stamped below the horizon or booked into any month other than its instant's, so the rule binds every writer. A month reading attempts the delivered non-waiting closure, raising the horizon when granted and otherwise reading it as stored, and reads final only when the horizon is at or after the first instant of the next month; otherwise it reads not yet final, stating that operations may still be booked into it. The capability boundary evaluates budgets for the month of the instant the recorder returned. The rule governs every month reading over the operation record and its partitions; throughput is unchanged.
- Scope of impact: M-001 adds the check in the sixth resource; M-003 stamps and books; M-004 passes the booked period to the delivered evaluator; M-007 and M-012 decide finality; M-025 re-points the delivered checks; M-040 is unchanged.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-003, stating on every month reading that it may still change, with no datastore rule.
- Benefits: no check and no change to the recorder, the capability boundary or the fixtures.
- Risks: no month reading over the operation record, budget utilisation included, is ever final.
- Why not selected: it ties O-002 on impact surface and loses on reuse leverage, because it reuses neither the record horizon nor the stamping discipline.

2. Refusing an operation booked into a closed month without restamping it, considered within O-002.
- Benefits: the occurrence instant stays the process clock's.
- Risks: an operation whose cost was already incurred at a provider would be refused and its record lost, breaking exactly-once accounting.
- Why not selected: the datastore stamp books a late operation into the open month instead of losing it.

3. O-001, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008 and O-009, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: once the horizon passes a month's end no operation can be booked into it by any writer, so a month reading presented as final never changes; one rule covers every month reading over the operation record.
- Tradeoffs accepted: an operation committing after the horizon passed its occurrence month is booked into the next month; delivered checks recording at fixed instants read the booked period back; a month reading may write the horizon row while closing.
- Risks introduced: R-005, delivered fixture checks; R-008, readings not final longer under sustained traffic; R-009, booking month versus occurrence month.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of operations recorded below the horizon in force when they committed; the expected value is zero, refused by the check.
  - The count of month readings presented as final whose figure differs on a later read; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of alerts raised against a month other than the operation's booked month; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - In a throwaway store, an operation committed after a reading taken before the horizon passed the month, read as not yet final, and a reading presented as final unchanged after a further operation booked into the open month.
  - A direct insert stamped below the horizon refused by the check.
  - Review of every month reading over the operation record against the rule.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if booking by the datastore's instant is rejected; the check is dropped, the recorder restored, and every month reading states the limit as O-003 would.
