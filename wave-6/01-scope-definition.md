## Metadata

- Feature name: The multi-channel capability, and the instrumentation that would make the approval-workload re-examination decidable
- Requested by: the CEO and sole owner, through the supplied feature request `tasks/MC-7/input.md`, read together with the owner's decision on channel isolation recorded 2026-10-08 in the repository's CEO decision record
- Business goal: bringing a further channel into being becomes a recorded owner decision plus configuration and nothing else, and the owner can take the approval-workload re-examination that must precede any second channel on what the company's own records observed rather than on assumption
- Target outcome: every per-channel matter the company's shared workforce depends on is held per channel and re-pointable, the payee is one company-level fact, every analytics, budget and approval-effort reading partitions by channel inside the three measurement cases, and the eight carried items the request names are closed or stated as structural limits
- Scope decision date: 2026-10-08

## Business Context

- Problem statement: the company is planned to run several channels on one shared workforce, but today a channel carries only its platform, language and registration flag, every analytics and approval reading is channel-agnostic or single-channel by construction, the payment-account condition is recorded per channel although the owner has now settled that one payee serves every channel, and the approval-workload question that must be answered before any second channel cannot be read per channel from the company's own records; several carried defects also leave the entry point unreachable on real recorded state and some month readings open to later change.
- Value hypothesis: if a second channel is configuration rather than construction, the owner's decision to add one costs no further development and carries no unexamined structural risk, and if every reading the re-examination needs is already partitioned and labelled by its measurement case, the re-examination can be taken the moment a real approval series exists instead of waiting on another build.
- Affected users: the CEO and sole owner, who approves every publication individually, decides whether and when a second channel exists and carries the shared-payee enforcement exposure; the shared operator and agent roles that plan, budget and report across channels; and the later change that first publishes, which inherits whichever readings this one partitioned.
- Success measure: every reading the company publishes for a period is readable per channel and resolves to exactly one of observed, observed zero or unmeasured; a fixture second channel in a self-dropping demonstration store is brought into being and fully partitioned with no source change and stays not live; the payment-account condition cannot differ between channels; and each of the eight carried items reads closed or carries its limit structurally in the change account.
