# Architecture Decision Record D-003

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-003
- Title: Every published reading partitions by channel through a relation its record already carries, with the cases decided at one site and the company figure computed from the same rows
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-7; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0007; planner tasks T-003, T-015, T-016, T-029 and T-033; sequencing constraints P-006 and P-007

## Context

- Problem statement: every analytics, cost and throughput reading the company publishes is channel-agnostic or single-channel by construction; each must become readable per channel and for the company inside the closed three-case union, with a channel known to the register and found empty reading differently from a channel or period never observed, and with additive company figures equal to the sum of their channels.
- Business and technical constraints: C-004 keeps every quantity in the union with the membership assertion covering every added type; C-009 requires exact additivity with money arithmetic in the datastore; C-010 keeps revenue-derived figures dark; C-011 forbids re-creating a delivered reading; C-012 places the month readings under the finality rule.
- Current architecture baseline: F-002 records the mandatory channel references on items, units and operation rows; F-011 and F-012 record the delivered cost rule and the period selection; F-016 and F-017 record the lifecycle subject format and the channel-free throughput count; F-025 records the dossier reader's recorded channel; F-029 records the union and the membership assertion.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: every published reading is listed with its record and the relation to a channel: operation-record readings use the row's channel identifier; item-scoped readings use the item's channel reference; throughput uses the unit named by the declared lifecycle subject format and the unit's channel reference; revenue-derived figures read a register with no channel and stay dark for every channel and for the company. A new channel partition read port returns one boundary summary per channel identifier present in the register or in the rows, each the datastore's aggregation, with the company figure computed in the same statement over the same rows. A channel identifier absent from the register forms its own named partition and an entry naming no unit is reported unattributed, so no row leaves the sum. At one composing site, a channel absent from the register reads unmeasured naming the register, a month ending at or before the record's earliest entry reads unmeasured naming the record's start, a count otherwise reads observed with observed zero included, and a cost figure follows the delivered rule. The delivered company-only members are kept beside the partitioned ones.
- Scope of impact: M-008 declares the port; M-007 implements it; M-011 holds the read models; M-012 decides the cases; M-013 and M-014 publish them; M-023 extends the membership assertion.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-006, a channel column written on every audit entry for the throughput partition.
- Benefits: a typed relation instead of a parsed subject.
- Risks: the append-only entry and its draft change shape, every appender must supply it, and every entry written before the change reads no channel.
- Why not selected: one more contract change than O-002 on the second criterion, and the delivered subject already carries the unit.

2. Adding per-channel members to the delivered read ports, considered within O-002.
- Benefits: one port per record.
- Risks: every delivered implementer and test double of those ports changes, which is a contract change per port.
- Why not selected: new port interfaces add the members without changing a delivered contract.

3. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-007, O-008 and O-009, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: every reading partitions over a relation the records already carry, so no record changes shape; the company figure equals the sum of its channels by construction; the two zero-like cases stay distinct per channel.
- Tradeoffs accepted: throughput relates entries to channels by the declared subject format; per-channel cost with no operation stays unmeasured under the delivered rule, with the observed-zero case applying to counts.
- Risks introduced: R-010, the two cases collapsing; R-011, a channel absent from the register; R-012, a subject outside the declared format; R-025, a different reading of the per-channel cost case; R-031, the coverage marker misread.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The difference between each additive company figure and the sum of its channel figures; the expected value is exactly zero.
  - The count of published readings with no per-channel form; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of per-channel quantities outside the union; the expected value is zero, refused by the solution build.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Two fixture channels with recorded operations in a throwaway store, comparing company and channel figures.
  - A registered channel with no operation beside an unregistered channel and an unobserved month, read side by side.
  - A seeded analytics member outside the union failing the solution build naming the offending type.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed reading by reading: removing a partitioned member leaves the delivered company member unchanged, since the partitioned members are additive.
