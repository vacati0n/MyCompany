# Architecture Decision Record D-002

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-002
- Title: The payment-account condition is resolved for every channel from one company-level observation record, and per-channel payment-account rows are retained unread
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-7; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0007; planner tasks T-002, T-014 and T-028; sequencing constraints P-001 and P-004

## Context

- Problem statement: the owner decided on 2026-10-08 that one legal entity with one payee and one payment account serves every channel, so the payee must be a company-level fact no channel can differ on, yet the payment-account condition is recorded per channel today, and the observations already recorded must not be lost.
- Business and technical constraints: C-007 forbids any record shape or key admitting a new per-channel payee or payment-account value; C-002 keeps all three conditions undischarged and refusing from recorded state; C-003 forbids recording any condition in the company's own store; C-015 requires additive change with every row keeping its meaning.
- Current architecture baseline: F-006 records the per-channel condition table, the gate ledger's per-channel read and the register keeping the latest observation per condition; F-002 records each channel's mandatory company reference; F-004 records that no production code writes a condition.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: a company-level payment-account observation record holds the delivered three-valued state, mandatory evidence and observation date, keyed by company and date, created empty. The gate ledger builds the delivered condition register from the channel's observations of library registration and two-step verification and from the company's observations of the payment account, reached through the channel's company reference, so every channel of a company resolves that condition from the same rows. The per-channel condition table gains a check, validated for new rows only, refusing a new payment-account row; every row already recorded is retained and no longer read for resolution, so nothing is lost and resolution can only become more refusing. Each channel's risk profile carries a required authored statement that the channel shares its company's single payee and payment account with every other channel of the company, naming how many channels the register holds for it; no key reaches the statement. The other two conditions stay per channel and unchanged.
- Scope of impact: M-001 creates the record and the check in the sixth resource; M-005 reads it; M-010 and M-019 carry the statement; M-028, the condition register, is unchanged.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-001, keeping the payment account per channel with a datastore check refusing a state that differs between channels of one company.
- Benefits: no new table and no change to the gate ledger's read.
- Risks: a per-channel value still exists, two channels differ between writes, and the check reads rows of several channels.
- Why not selected: it violates C-007 and is eliminated.

2. Copying every per-channel payment-account observation forward into the company-level record, considered within O-002.
- Benefits: the company record would start with the recorded history.
- Risks: two channels' observations of one date and different states would have to be reconciled by choosing one, which decides a fact the owner never recorded.
- Why not selected: retaining the rows unread loses nothing and decides nothing, and leaves the condition at least as refusing.

3. O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008 and O-009, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: the payment-account condition cannot differ between channels of a company; the shared payee is stated on every channel's risk profile; no recorded observation is lost.
- Tradeoffs accepted: a per-channel payment-account row recorded before the change stops counting, so the owner records the company-level observation when the single account exists.
- Risks introduced: R-003, retained per-channel rows in the company's store; R-004, a reader treating a retained row as current; R-029, a different corporate structure recorded later.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of new per-channel payment-account rows admitted; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of channels of one company whose payment-account resolution differs; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of retained per-channel payment-account rows reported when the resource is applied; reported, never deleted.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Two fixture channels of one company read the payment-account condition identically before and after one company-level observation is recorded in a throwaway store.
  - A per-channel payment-account insert refused by the added check.
  - Each fixture channel's risk profile reading the shared-payee statement.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the owner records a corporate structure other than one legal entity; the company-level record and the check are dropped while the record is empty, and restore-to-point applies once it holds an observation.
