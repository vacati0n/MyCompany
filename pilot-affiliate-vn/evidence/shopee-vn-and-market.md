# Evidence: Shopee Affiliate Vietnam programme rules and the VN content-commerce market

Access date for every source below: **2026-10-26**.

**Labels**
- **[FIRST-PARTY]**: a page published by Shopee (help.shopee.vn, shopee.vn/blog).
- **[THIRD-PARTY]**: a press article or research-firm citation.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no first-party page was found and the third-party sources are weak or disagree.

**Quotes** are verbatim in the original language and under 25 words, with an English gloss after the arrow. Where a [THIRD-PARTY] item has no verbatim text, it is marked "(press summary)".

**Confidence** is H, M or L.

**How the sources were reached**
- affiliate.shopee.vn pages need a login, and shopee.vn/affiliate/* returned 503 or a rate-limit page. The terms were therefore read from the help-centre copy.
- The help-centre affiliate portal is https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10.
- Nothing was signed up for, logged into or submitted.

## Q1. Eligibility, account form, IDs, tax code and payout

**1.1 [FIRST-PARTY] The current terms are dated 08/07/2026.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/122944
- Quote: "được đăng tải vào ngày 08/07/2026, có hiệu lực sau 07 (bảy) ngày kể từ ngày đăng tải"
  → published 08/07/2026, effective 7 days later.
- Confidence: H

**1.2 [FIRST-PARTY] Who may take part.**
- Four participant types exist: Cá Nhân, Cá Nhân Kinh Doanh, Hộ Kinh Doanh and Doanh Nghiệp (individual, business individual, household business, company).
- The KOL/KOC Affiliate form is closed to companies.
- URL: same as 1.1
- Quote: "Doanh Nghiệp: Không thể tham gia" (in the KOL/KOC row)
  → companies cannot join the KOL/KOC form.
- Other forms in the same table:
  - "KOL Affiliate Doanh Nghiệp": household business or company, with a daily order-volume threshold and a service contract.
  - MCN: household business or company.
  - Seller Affiliate: open to all types.
  - Short-tail: companies only.
- Implication: if the company itself joins as a Doanh Nghiệp, it cannot use the ordinary KOL/KOC form. It would need the KOL DN or MCN route, which has thresholds and a contract.
- Confidence: H

**1.3 [FIRST-PARTY] One account, one form.**
- URL: same as 1.1
- Quote 1: "Đối Tác TTLK chỉ được tham gia Chương Trình TTLK dưới 01 (một) trong các hình thức"
  → a partner may join under only one form.
- Quote 2: "chỉ có thể có 1 (một) Tài Khoản TTLK"
  → only one affiliate account.
- Confidence: H

**1.4 [FIRST-PARTY] Age.**
- Under-18s need a parent's or guardian's permission.
- URL: same as 1.1
- Quote: "Nếu bạn dưới 18 tuổi … phải được sự cho phép của cha mẹ hoặc người giám hộ hợp pháp"
  → under 18 needs a parent's or guardian's permission.
- The Shopee blog says the business-individual form is for people aged 18 and over (https://shopee.vn/blog/tiep-thi-lien-ket-shopee-la-gi/): "có độ tuổi từ 18 trở lên" → aged 18 or over.
- Confidence: H for the terms, M for the blog. The blog disclaims accuracy.

**1.5 [FIRST-PARTY] Follower thresholds. KOC has no minimum.**
- URL: same as 1.1
- KOL quote: "có ít nhất 5.000 người theo dõi / đăng ký trên TikTok / YouTube; hoặc … 10.000 người theo dõi trên Facebook / Instagram"
  → at least 5k on TikTok/YouTube or 10k on Facebook/Instagram.
- KOC quote: "Những đối tác không đáp ứng tiêu chí trở thành KOL Affiliate sẽ trở thành KOC Affiliate"
  → anyone below the KOL bar becomes a KOC.
- Implication: a new faceless channel can join as a KOC from day one.
- Confidence: H

**1.6 [FIRST-PARTY] Default form, and conversion.**
- A new account is recorded as an individual by default.
- It can convert to Cá Nhân Kinh Doanh (procedure in article 203394).
- URL: same as 1.1
- Quote: "hình thức ban đầu … sẽ được mặc định ghi nhận là cá nhân"
  → the initial form defaults to individual.
- Confidence: H

**1.7 [FIRST-PARTY] Channels must be registered and approved, and are reviewed monthly.**
- Promotion is allowed only on channels that were registered and approved.
- The terms list Facebook, Snapchat, Zalo, Instagram, Pinterest and Twitter "được chấp thuận bởi Shopee" (approved by Shopee).
- The blog lists "TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter)".
- URLs: same as 1.1; https://shopee.vn/blog/tiep-thi-lien-ket-shopee-la-gi/
- Quote: "Shopee tiến hành kiểm duyệt Tài Khoản TTLK … mỗi tháng"
  → Shopee reviews affiliate accounts monthly.
- Article 134926 (https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/134926) adds that linked channels are checked at approval and periodically against Community Standards, including for ownership and copied content.
- Confidence: H

**1.8 [FIRST-PARTY] ID and tax code are mandatory for individuals.**
- URL: same as 1.1
- Quote: "họ và tên, CCCD, số tài khoản ngân hàng, mã số thuế"
  → full name, national ID (CCCD), bank account, tax code.
- Business individuals must provide an invoice from an authorised third party.
- Companies must provide a VAT invoice and are paid within 30 working days.
- Confidence: H

**1.9 [FIRST-PARTY] The payment profile must match the ID exactly.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/180808
- Requirements:
  - account type "Cá nhân trong nước" (domestic individual);
  - the information must match the CCCD 100%;
  - the bank account must be in the same name;
  - a 12-digit CCCD with photos of both sides;
  - a screenshot of the tax-code (MST) lookup.
- Implication: an individual account pays out only to that person's own bank account. Revenue cannot flow straight to the company.
- Confidence: H

**1.10 [FIRST-PARTY] Payout minimum is 10,000 VND.**
- URL: same as 1.1
- Quote: "mức chi trả tối thiểu là 10.000 VND"
  → minimum payout 10,000 VND.
- If payment information is still missing 6 months after the first revenue, Shopee may refuse to pay.
- Confidence: H

**1.11 [FIRST-PARTY] Individuals are paid twice a week from November 2025.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/188941
- Quote: "thay đổi từ hai tuần một lần sang hai lần mỗi tuần"
  → changed from every two weeks to twice a week.
- How it works:
  - Commission is calculated daily on the previous day's completed valid orders.
  - Orders completed Sunday to Tuesday are paid the following Tuesday.
  - Orders completed Wednesday to Saturday are paid the following Thursday.
  - Businesses are paid twice a month. Short-tail is paid monthly.
- Payment follows order *completion*, which comes after delivery, not order placement.
- Confidence: H

**1.12 [FIRST-PARTY] Video and Live are not separate sign-ups.**
- PPV (Shopee KOL Video) and PPLS (KOL Livestream) are invitation-only campaign modes inside the same affiliate programme.
- Shopee offers them by email ("[Shopee KOL Booking Video]") for a fixed fee.
- They are open to KOL/KOC, KOL DN and MCN.
- Shopee Video itself carries Xtra commission (see 2.7).
- URL: same as 1.1
- Confidence: M-H. The structure is clear. How often invitations reach small KOCs is unknown.

**1.13 [FIRST-PARTY] Joining is free.**
- URL: https://shopee.vn/blog/tiep-thi-lien-ket-shopee-la-gi/
- Quote: "không mất phí"
  → no fee to join.
- Confidence: M (blog)

## Q2. Commission, attribution, voids, caps, fees and tax

**2.1 [FIRST-PARTY] Commission is calculated on net order value.**
- Net value is after seller, Shopee and third-party discounts and Shopee Xu.
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/122944
- Quote: "Giá Trị Thuần Đơn Hàng Thành Công x tỷ lệ hoa hồng theo ngành hàng do Shopee quyết định tại từng thời điểm"
  → net successful-order value × the category rate Shopee sets from time to time.
- There are three commission types:
  - Cơ Bản (base), paid by Shopee;
  - Xtra, paid by the seller;
  - Thưởng (bonus).
- Confidence: H

**2.2 [FIRST-PARTY] Rates vary by category and channel, and the table is login-gated.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/123037
- Quote: "theo từng thời điểm, cho từng ngành hàng … và từng kênh truyền thông"
  → per period, per category and per media channel.
- The actual table is at affiliate.shopee.vn/offer/shopee_offer, which redirects to login. It was **not read**.
- The rate is fixed at the moment the order is placed (article 122941).
- Confidence: H that the rates are variable. No first-party numbers were obtained.

**2.3 [THIRD-PARTY] Example rates and a per-order cap.**
- URL: https://ghn.vn/blogs/tip-ban-hang/muc-hoa-hong-tiep-thi-lien-ket-shopee (updated 25/10/2025)
- Quote: "Mức hoa hồng tối đa nhận được cho mỗi đơn hàng là 30.000 VNĐ"
  → maximum commission per order is 30,000 VND.
- The page also gives women's fashion at 3.68%.
- Other blogs show 2025 tables that split new and existing buyers, for example bags at 13.4% and phones at 4.7%.
- First-party support for a cap: Shopee advertises "hoa hồng không giới hạn đối với các sản phẩm hoa hồng Xtra" → unlimited commission on Xtra products (article 122941). This implies the *base* commission is capped.
- Confidence: L-M. The cap value is UNVERIFIED first-party.

**2.4 [FIRST-PARTY] Attribution: a 7-day cookie and a 30-day completion window.**
- URLs: same as 2.1; https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/122941
- Cookie quote: "Thời gian lưu giữ Cookies là 7 (bảy) ngày cho cả ứng dụng di động Shopee và website"
  → the cookie lasts 7 days on app and web.
- Order quote: "Người mua cần thao tác đặt hàng thành công trong vòng 7 ngày sau khi nhấp vào Link"
  → the buyer must place the order within 7 days of the click.
- The terms define a successful order as completed "trong thời gian 30 (ba mươi) ngày" (within 30 days) of the visit.
- Reading: the order must be placed within 7 days and completed within 30.
- Confidence: H

**2.5 [FIRST-PARTY] Cancellations, refusals and returns void commission.**
- URL: same as 2.1
- Quote: "đơn hàng bị huỷ hoặc bị … từ chối nhận hàng hoặc bị trả hàng"
  → cancelled, refused or returned orders are excluded.
- Fraud, bot orders and orders linked next to prohibited content are also excluded.
- Return rates therefore cut revenue directly.
- Confidence: H

**2.6 [FIRST-PARTY] Self-purchase, collusion and buying for resale are clawed back.**
- URL: same as 2.1
- Quote: "Đối Tác TTLK mua sản phẩm thông qua Siêu Liên Kết"
  → the affiliate buying via their own link is excluded.
- Also excluded: purchases made "nhằm mục đích bán lại" (for resale).
- Confidence: H

**2.7 [FIRST-PARTY] Shopee Video Xtra commission goes "up to 35%".**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/164064
- Quote: "hoa hồng Xtra (lên tới 35%)"
  → Xtra commission up to 35%.
- Xtra is seller-funded and applies only to opted-in products. No Xtra is paid on links to the homepage or category pages (article 122941).
- Confidence: H that the ceiling is advertised. The typical rate is unknown.

**2.8 [FIRST-PARTY] Affiliates pay a 0.98% service fee, VAT included, from July 2025.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/174381
- Quote: "Phí Dịch Vụ: 0.98% trên tổng giá trị Đối tác được thụ hưởng từ Chương Trình Affiliate"
  → service fee of 0.98% of everything the partner receives.
- It is deducted each period, starting 16–31/7/2025.
- Confidence: H

**2.9 UNVERIFIED: the "5% technical support fee" is not an affiliate fee.**
- Third-party seller-tool sources describe it as a fee charged to cross-border *sellers* from February 2026.
- No first-party affiliate page mentions a 5% fee. The first-party affiliate fee is 0.98% (2.8).
- Confidence: M that it does not apply to affiliates.

**2.10 [FIRST-PARTY] Shopee withholds 10% personal income tax from individuals on payments of 250,000 VND or more, from 20/11/2025.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/196407
- Quote: "Từ ngày 20/11/2025: việc khấu trừ thuế sẽ được áp dụng cho các khoản thanh toán từ 250.000 VND/lần, với mức khấu trừ 10%"
  → from 20/11/2025, 10% is withheld on each payment of 250k VND or more.
- The previous threshold was 2,000,000 VND per payment.
- The terms (122944) confirm that Shopee withholds, declares and pays this for non-business individuals.
- Confidence: H

**2.11 [FIRST-PARTY] Business individuals and household businesses: 5% VAT plus 5% personal income tax, withheld by Shopee.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/163104
- Quote: "Thuế suất thuế GTGT: 5%" … "Thuế suất thuế TNCN: 5%"
  → VAT 5%, PIT 5%.
- The page also covers:
  - the 1 billion VND per year revenue threshold;
  - Form 01/TKN-CNKD, due by 31/1;
  - Nghị định 254/2026/NĐ-CP;
  - that Shopee does **not** do the annual settlement.
- Confidence: M-H. How the threshold interacts with withholding needs a tax adviser.

**2.12 [FIRST-PARTY] Disputes must be raised within 24 hours.**
- Individuals have 24 hours to dispute the commission result. After that it is deemed accepted.
- URL: same as 2.1
- Confidence: H

**2.13 [FIRST-PARTY] Some categories pay no commission at all.**
- Excluded: infant formula, baby vitamins and supplements, pacifiers, baby bottles, OTC medicines, and alcohol above 15%.
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/132472
- Quote: "Bất kỳ đơn hàng phát sinh từ ngành hàng, sản phẩm này đều sẽ không được ghi nhận hoa hồng."
  → orders from these categories earn no commission.
- Confidence: H

**2.14 Net take-home, worked example.**
This is derived from 2.8 and 2.10 and is not a source.
- Starting point: an individual earns 1,000,000 VND of gross commission in one payout.
- Service fee: 0.98% leaves about 990,200 VND.
- Personal income tax: 10% withheld (payout ≥ 250k) leaves about **891,000 VND, roughly 89% of gross**.
- The order in which the fee and the tax are applied was not confirmed first-party.

## Q3. Content and link rules, AI and bans

**3.1 [FIRST-PARTY] Affiliates may not promote sellers on competitor platforms, TikTok named.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/122944
- Quote: "quảng bá bất kỳ các sản phẩm … của các Người Bán trên các sàn thương mại điện tử khác và đang là đối thủ của Shopee (bao gồm … Tiktok, Lazada, Tiki, Sendo)"
  → may not promote products of sellers on competing marketplaces, including TikTok, Lazada, Tiki and Sendo.
- **Key risk for a dual TikTok Shop + Shopee pilot.** Promoting TikTok Shop sellers from the same affiliate identity or channel may breach the Shopee terms. The exact reach of the clause (the same channel? the same person?) is not defined. A legal read is needed.
- Confidence: H that the clause exists. Its scope is uncertain.

**3.2 [FIRST-PARTY] Prohibited traffic and promotion methods.**
- URL: same as 3.1
- Prohibited:
  - email, SMS or ZNS without written approval;
  - SEM or keyword bidding on "Shopee";
  - layer, iFrame, pop-up and pop-under ads, auto-redirects, cookie dropping and post-view attribution;
  - contests and lotteries;
  - copying Shopee's logos, images or content;
  - scraping;
  - buying to resell;
  - negative comments about Shopee.
- Confidence: H

**3.3 [FIRST-PARTY] No doctor imagery for food or supplements, and no unsupported superlatives.**
- URL: same as 3.1
- Quote: "sử dụng … hình ảnh … bác sĩ, dược sĩ … để … quảng cáo đối với thực phẩm và/hoặc thực phẩm chức năng"
  → using images of doctors or pharmacists to advertise food or supplements is prohibited.
- Words such as "nhất", "duy nhất", "tốt nhất" and "số một" (most, only, best, number one) are banned without legal evidence.
- Affiliates must hold any required licences and follow advertising law: "đã có và sẽ duy trì tất cả các giấy phép … quy định về quảng cáo" → holds and maintains all licences … advertising rules.
- Confidence: H

**3.4 [FIRST-PARTY] Anti-fraud policy: stolen content, link-only posts, mass video creation and multiple accounts are banned.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/199468 (05/09/2024)
- Quote (item n): "trộm cắp hoặc sử dụng nội dung của người khác và đăng tải công khai … mà không được sự cho phép"
  → stealing or using others' content without permission.
- Also banned:
  - (j) random content and clickbait;
  - (k) links with no content;
  - (o) fake or proxy orders;
  - (s) buying, selling or renting accounts;
  - (t) tools that fake views, clicks or orders, or that "tạo video hàng loạt" (mass-produce videos);
  - (v) multiple accounts;
  - (w) emulators;
  - (z) mass chat messages.
- Fine: up to 10,000,000 VND per act.
- Confidence: H

**3.5 [FIRST-PARTY] AI videos must be labelled "Video tạo bởi AI"; unlabelled AI videos posted from 23/09/2026 are deleted.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/202687
- The rule cites Luật Trí tuệ nhân tạo 134/2025/QH15, effective 01/03/2026.
- Quote: unlabelled AI videos "sẽ bị xóa" → will be deleted.
- Also banned:
  - AI-made fake reviews;
  - realistic AI minors;
  - celebrities without consent;
  - "Sử dụng cùng một AI avatar trên nhiều tài khoản khác nhau" → using the same AI avatar on several accounts;
  - repetitive content.
- Penalties escalate: reduced reach, then deletion, then a temporary or permanent Shopee Video ban.
- Confidence: H

**3.6 [FIRST-PARTY] Shopee Video: the product shown must match, and low quality is penalised.**
- URL: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/10/article/164064
- The cart is removed if the video does not show the tagged product.
- Low-quality videos can get the video feature banned.
- Confidence: H

**3.7 [FIRST-PARTY] Accounts are terminated after 6 months with no results, and for identity fraud.**
- URL: same as 3.1
- Quote: "không phát sinh Kết Quả TTLK trong vòng 6 (sáu) tháng liên tục"
  → no affiliate results for 6 consecutive months.
- Other termination triggers:
  - multiple accounts on one device;
  - false ID, tax code or bank details;
  - automated registration;
  - unfair competition.
- Penalties:
  - liquidated damages of 30% of amounts paid;
  - up to 10,000,000 VND per fraud act;
  - pending payouts retained.
- Confidence: H

**3.8 GAP: disclosure.**
- No first-party clause requiring #ad or "tiếp thị liên kết" disclosure was found.
- The only identification rule is that emails and SMS must say they come from the affiliate, not from Shopee.
- Confidence: M that no such clause exists in the terms. Vietnamese advertising law may still require it, which is out of scope here.

**3.9 GAP: seller images and videos.**
- No explicit permission to reuse seller-supplied product images or video was found.
- The only rules are the bans on using "others' content without permission" (3.4) and on copying Shopee content (3.2).
- Treat re-uploading seller video as **risky** unless the seller grants permission.
- Confidence: M
