### RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1. **Eligibility and onboarding, per platform (Vietnam).** Age, residency, identity verification (CCCD / eKYC), follower or activity minimums, account type (personal vs business), tax code requirement, payout method and currency, payout threshold and timing. What the CEO must do personally; what an agent may never do (create accounts, handle credentials).
2. **Commission and fees.** First-party commission structure by category (open vs targeted/seller-set plans on TikTok Shop; Shopee's category rates and extra commissions), attribution window, what voids a commission (returns, cancellations, self-purchase), platform fees and deductions. Every figure dated.
3. **Content rules.** Each platform's rules for affiliate/shoppable content in Vietnam: disclosure labelling, unoriginal or reused content, AI-generated content labelling, what product footage and claims are allowed (health, cosmetics, supplements, children's products), posting limits, link and live-stream rules, and what gets an account restricted.
4. **Vietnamese law.** The affiliate/advertising disclosure obligations under the Law on Advertising and its amendments, e-commerce decrees and consumer-protection rules as they apply to an individual affiliate; personal income tax on affiliate commissions (who withholds, at what rate, thresholds) and any household-business registration trigger. Where first-party text cannot settle a point, say so and name it for local counsel; do not infer law.
5. **Content form options.** At least three, each costed and risk-rated against question 3: (a) real product footage shot by a person (samples: free sample programmes, buying, returning); (b) graphics drawn by code from product images and recorded product facts, reusing the delivered pipeline; (c) a mix. For each: what the platforms' originality rules say, what top-earning Vietnamese affiliate content of that form looks like (observed, cited), the CEO's time per video, and production cost per video. No generative model is assumed; if an option needs one, say so and price it separately.
6. **Product niche.** Two to four candidate categories for the pilot ranked on first-party commission, demand evidence, return/refund risk, claim and regulatory risk, and fit with options in question 5.
7. **Economics.** Break-even per month as a formula and as an ESTIMATE with stated assumptions (views per video, click-through, conversion, average order value, commission net of fees and tax), against the existing monthly budget (USD 77.41, metered ceiling USD 34.42). Which assumptions have no source; how the pilot would measure them in its first 30 days.
8. **What `CEO-D-1300` supersedes.** For `D-005`, `D-007`, `D-008`, `D-022` and `D-021`: keep, amend or supersede, each with its reason, for the owner to rule. What of the delivered pipeline the pilot reuses, and what Waves 12/13 would contribute if unfrozen.
