## 2. Description

> A honey bee that finds a resource two kilometres away returns to a dark hive and transmits its
> direction and distance to bees that have never seen it. This is an account of how that signal is
> built: the angle of the waggle run against gravity standing for the bearing against the sun, the
> duration of the run standing for accumulated optic flow, and the measured error in both.
>
> Karl von Frisch decoded the dance over roughly four decades and shared the 1973 Nobel Prize in
> Physiology or Medicine for it. What is covered here is the encoding itself — what is measured,
> in what units, and how badly.
>
> Sources are named on screen and listed below.
>
> von Frisch, *The Dance Language and Orientation of Bees* (Harvard University Press, 1967).
> Michelsen, Andersen, Storm, Kirchner & Lindauer, *Behavioral Ecology and Sociobiology* (1992).
