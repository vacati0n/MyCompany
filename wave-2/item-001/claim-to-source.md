## 2. The footage-removal assessment (`A-010`)

**Judgement: the argument survives.** All 23 claims are made by the narration and shown by an
original graphic. No claim rests on a licensed clip; the clips show what bees and flowers look
like, which is illustration, not evidence.

Stated as the test the originality position actually sets: with all 18 licensed clips deleted,
this item is still a complete essay on a mechanism, with its diagrams intact. That is the standard
`D-005` sets, and it is the standard that matters, because licensing does not cure reused content
— the governing policy names collections assembled from other people's work as not allowed *"even
if you have their permission"*. The originality burden rests entirely on the script, the
narration and the original graphics, and here it is carried by all three.

---

## 3. Two deliberate restraints in the claim set

**No precise figure is asserted where the literature does not support one.** The narration says
the one-second-per-kilometre conversion "is often quoted" and immediately qualifies it with
`CL-12`, rather than presenting it as a constant. A number stated flatly here would be a claim the
operator could not defend if challenged.

**Contested questions are presented as contested.** `CL-22` is the clearest case: the narration
states both readings of the dance scatter and says the matter is unresolved. Presenting the
adaptive reading as settled would be the more satisfying script and the less defensible one.

## 4. Verification burden carried forward

Each source above is named to paper, journal and year, which is what `A-011` requires. What this
record does **not** contain is a first-hand re-read of each paper against the claim it supports:
the claims are stated from the established literature, and a reviewer with library access should
confirm `CL-04`, `CL-12` and `CL-20` in particular, because those three are the claims whose
quantitative content is most easily overstated in secondary sources.

Recorded here rather than left implicit, so the reviewer knows where to spend the effort.
