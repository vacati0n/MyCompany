## 3. Generated cutaways — zero

No generated cutaway is commissioned, so no recorded cutaway reason is owed under `A-009` and the
count of generated material carrying no reason is zero by construction rather than by inspection.

Two reasons, both stronger than cost. Generated "wildlife" cut into a documentary as though it
were recorded footage is a factual-integrity failure on a science item, and it is the documented
way this format fails. And commissioning none keeps the synthetic-media determination clean:
there is no generated footage of a real place or event to disclose.
