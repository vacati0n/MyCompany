# Architecture Decision Record D-002

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-002
- Title: The report period is the UTC Monday-based week resolved by the datastore, month-bound lines read every booking month the week touches, and finality is the stored horizon against the period's end
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-002, T-017 and T-023; sequencing constraints P-004 and P-007

## Context

- Problem statement: the delivered report takes its month from the process clock, no week or zone is recorded anywhere, and a week can span a month end while spend is governed by booking month.
- Business and technical constraints: C-004 the datastore's clock; C-008 no invented quantity; C-001 one snapshot.
- Current architecture baseline: F-003 records the process-clock month; F-006 the UTC booking month; F-007 the finality rule; F-027 the master plan's Monday-to-Saturday production week.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the period is the half-open UTC week from Monday 00:00; without an argument it is the week containing the report instant, with one it is the named ISO week, its bounds computed by the datastore in the read's first statement; each month-bound line reads every booking month the week touches, labelled with its month and that month's finality; a week is final where the stored horizon is at or after its end; hold ages are the instant less the held operation's instant.
- Scope of impact: M-003 computes the bounds; M-010 labels month lines; M-015 and M-016 take the month and week from the datastore.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009 and O-010, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record; the alternatives to the week rule itself are the zone and boundary the Design Gate may choose under Q-001

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: the period cannot move with the process clock, and no spend is shown against a month it was not booked in.
- Tradeoffs accepted: a week spanning a month end shows two month lines; the owner's local zone is not the period's zone.
- Risks introduced: R-005, a different ruling; R-008, a month end.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of period bounds or instants that move with a process clock skewed a day either way; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of month-bound lines without their month and finality; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - A skewed-clock demonstration in a demonstration store.
  - A week spanning a month end, each month read and labelled.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if omn-tech-lead rules another zone or boundary at the Design Gate; only the first statement's bounds change.
