## Metadata

- Feature name: The AI-management capability: weekly executive reports, rule-based recommendations, the open-decisions register and a read-only CEO dashboard, as code over recorded data, with no executive model call
- Requested by: the CEO and sole owner, through the supplied feature request `tasks/MC-9/input.md`, read together with the owner's three decisions of 2026-10-09 recorded at the end of the CEO decision record in the feature worktree: against an executive model call in this wave, setting ten comparable runs per task as the evidence threshold, and making the USD 34.42 metered allotment the company cost ceiling
- Business goal: the owner can take the weekly review of the company in 10 to 15 minutes from one consistent account of what the company's records show, seeing for every figure whether it was observed, observed as zero, never measured or merely recorded, and seeing which owner decisions and questions are still open, without paying for any model call
- Target outcome: the COO, CTO and CFO weekly reports, channel performance and the risk summary, each line from one read at one datastore instant with the period's finality stated; recommendations that are fixed, labelled rules over those readings and abstain over an unmeasured one; an open-decisions register read from a recorded source; a CEO brief and a read-only dashboard over the same read; and the four items carried from the previous wave closed or made visible
- Scope decision date: 2026-10-09

## Business Context

- Problem statement: the owner is the company's only decision-maker and must review its position weekly, but today the company's records are readable only as separate readings taken at different instants, through an operator command whose period comes from the process clock; there is no weekly COO, CTO or CFO view, no channel performance or risk summary, no recommendation of any kind, no reader for the cost controller's recorded decisions, and the open owner decisions and questions exist only as prose in a decision record and wave reports; meanwhile every production, quality, approval-minutes and revenue quantity is unmeasured because nothing has been produced, and a zero meaning none occurred could be mistaken for a zero meaning never measured.
- Value hypothesis: if the weekly review is composed by fixed code from one consistent read of the company's own records, every figure labelled by its measurement case and every recommendation a labelled rule that abstains where its reading is unmeasured, then the owner can rely on the review without a model call, the first real production records will populate it with no further build, and no owner decision will rest on a configured number or a fixture presented as an observation.
- Affected users: the CEO and sole owner, who reads the weekly review, decides every reserved matter and holds the USD 77.41 monthly budget; the operator roles that run the company's entry point; and the later change that records the first real production item, which inherits the readings this one makes reportable.
- Success measure: over a freshly installed demonstration store every unmeasurable line reads unmeasured and no recommendation is issued, each abstention saying why; over fixture records in a self-dropping demonstration store each report line, recommendation rule and dashboard tile shows the case and figure its recorded reading yields, all from one datastore instant; the open owner questions appear from the register and none from prose; and the owner reads the brief end to end within 15 minutes at the wave report.
