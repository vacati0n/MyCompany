# Architecture Decision Record D-004

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-004
- Title: Five pure report composers compose every line over the snapshot, each from its delivered composer where one exists, gate counts taken over the datastore-stamped audit actions
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-004, T-006, T-008, T-009, T-010, T-019, T-020, T-022, T-023, T-024 and T-025; sequencing constraints P-007

## Context

- Problem statement: no composer of the COO, CTO or CFO report, channel performance or the risk summary exists, several figures have no delivered reading, and gate transitions carry caller instants.
- Business and technical constraints: C-006 a delivered composer decides its line's case; C-007 the rule-determined assembly; C-008 no invented quantity; C-010 nothing discharged or published; C-014 every deferral listed.
- Current architecture baseline: F-019 lists the delivered composers; F-020 the figures no source records; F-021 the approval listing; F-022 the gate service's audit actions; F-023 the conditions and absences; F-013 the absent decision reader.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: M-010 holds one pure composer per report over the snapshot values; each line names its reading and composer; delivered readings are composed by their delivered composers; rates are counts of named audit actions over items reaching the step, unmeasured where the denominator is; figures no source records read unmeasured naming the absent source or not recorded; controller decisions and selection bases print exactly as recorded.
- Scope of impact: M-010 composes; M-012 carries the lines; M-024 is reused unchanged; M-028 and M-030 are read only.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-001, composing the same lines from the delivered readings' own transactions
- Benefits: no new reader
- Risks: lines at several instants
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-001

2. O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009 and O-010, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: every line's case is decided at one site and every line names what it rests on.
- Tradeoffs accepted: gate counts follow the audit instant, not the caller's transition instant.
- Risks introduced: R-003, a bypassing gate writer; R-008, a month end; R-011, recorded interim statements.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of lines deciding afresh a case a delivered composer decides; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of lines naming no reading; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Each report inspected over a fresh and a populated demonstration store.
  - Each line compared with its delivered composer over fixtures.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed for a line if its delivered composer is found unable to take the snapshot's rows; that line then reads unmeasured naming the gap until its composer is extended.
