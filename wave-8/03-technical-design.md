### 5.3 Selected Approach

- Selected: O-002, one read-only snapshot over every source with the horizon read as stored, an eighth resource for the register, the re-verification record, the held-outcome record and the widened reading columns, code-recorded programme facts, pure composers and rules, and a console dashboard.
- Structural change:
  - One read (D-001). M-003 opens one transaction of its own on the data source, repeatable read and read only, never through the unit of work (M-026). Its first statement reads, in one statement, the datastore's statement-start clock as the report instant, the horizon as stored, the earliest audit entry, and the period bounds of D-002 computed by the datastore from that instant; in the datastore that first statement fixes the snapshot (A-004), so the instant and the snapshot belong to the same statement, and every later statement takes the instant as a parameter rather than reading a clock again. Before the first statement the transaction sets a transaction-local lock bound of two seconds and a transaction-local statement bound of five seconds, so the only hold a plain read can meet, an exclusive structural hold while a resource is applied, ends the read with a named outcome rather than a wait; the whole read stays inside the delivered 30-second command timeout (F-009). No statement selects for share or update, takes an advisory hold or calls the closure (M-027), so no admission's shared horizon hold or scope hold, no recovery under its 120-second or unbounded timeout, no chain-head hold and no client-lost transaction is ever waited on, whatever its duration, and no admission or gate writer waits on the read (C-002). The sources, all in this snapshot: the channel register; the operation record's channel and company totals for the period and for every booking month it touches, with tier parts; the budgets and alerts of those months; dossier and stage evidence counts; the audit entries by action for the period, which carry the gate service's named actions (F-022, A-007) and the throughput counts; the job queue and unhandled failures; the latest gate state per item and the awaiting-approval listing (F-021); decided approvals and their measurements; the three conditions per channel; the revenue parameter register; the route register; the benchmark observations with their operations' cost; the admission decisions, readings and candidates booked in the period; the held-outcome record; the register; the policy statements and re-verification results. Code-recorded figures, the envelope, the ceiling, the thresholds, the owner's ten and the programme facts, are constants, identical at every instant. The read's named outcomes: a lock or statement bound reached ends as the read being unavailable while the store is being changed, naming the bound; a missing resource ends as the store lacking the eighth resource, naming it; any other datastore failure ends as the read failed, naming its datastore class; no serialisation conflict arises in a read-only repeatable-read transaction, and one, if raised, ends as the read failed with the class named (R-004). The read takes no closure, so finality is read, never made (A-002): a period is final where the stored horizon is at or after its end (F-007), which holds once any delivered closure has run after the period ends (A-012); otherwise it is stated not final, as of the stored horizon. Because every writer of a period-bounded figure stamps under the shared horizon hold and the horizon is raised only while none is in flight (F-005, F-006), every row of a final period is in the snapshot, and a final period re-reads identical. Every composed line carries the report instant.
  - Period (D-002). The proposed period is the UTC week from Monday 00:00 inclusive to the next Monday 00:00 exclusive, the same zone as the booking month (F-006), and Monday-based like the master plan's production week (F-027, A-006). Without an argument the week is the one containing the report instant, computed by the datastore in the first statement; with an argument the operator names an ISO week, and the datastore computes its bounds; the process clock is never read (C-004). Each month-bound line, CFO spend against the ceiling and the envelope, channel budget utilisation and controller thresholds, reads every booking month the week touches, one or two, each line labelled with its month and that month's own finality, month-to-date as of the instant where the month is not over; nothing is pro-rated (R-008). The week is final where the stored horizon is at or after its end; a non-final week states that its figures can still change and names the stored horizon. Hold ages are the report instant less the held operation's datastore instant.
  - Rendering (D-003). Every figure is a measurement quantity rendered by its delivered rendering or a recorded amount rendered by its own (F-017). A recorded figure that is not money, the owner's ten, a threshold percentage, a programme threshold or a dated change, takes a third shape of the recorded-amount type, carrying a decimal or a date, its unit and where it was recorded, rendered as recorded with that source and never as observed; the union stays at three cases (C-005). Each line renders as its label, its figure, its case word, the reading and composer it rests on, the instant and its finality; an unmeasured figure prints no amount, unit or zero, and an observed zero prints the zero with its unit and the word observed. The brief, the reports and the dashboard use this one line rendering, so a tile cannot render a line differently.
  - Report lines (D-004). M-010 holds one composer per report, each a pure function of the snapshot values returning lines, each naming the reading and composer it rests on. Where a delivered reading exists the line is that composer's output over the snapshot rows (C-006): operations and cost by channel, budgets with the channel budget and company ceiling, throughput coverage with the snapshot's stored-horizon coverage, dossier counts, approval listing and workload, stage outcomes, tier distribution and benchmark (F-019). The COO report: production lines are stage-completion and published counts from audit entries by action, planned items reading not recorded because no production plan is recorded (F-020), the committed rate never shown as planned, produced or published, buffer depth reading unmeasured naming the missing buffer record; quality lines are rejection, revision, copyright, policy and owner send-back rates, each a count of the named audit action over the count of items reaching that step, unmeasured where the denominator is unmeasured or zero, with every recorded send-back reason listed from the transitions those entries record; operations lines are queue length per stage, unhandled failures, held work and every deferred request from the held-outcome record with its reason, hold age and escalation state, those recorded before the eighth resource reading their escalation not recorded; channel lines are the per-channel awaiting-approval listing and published counts; finance lines are the period's booked cost and utilisation against the decided ceiling. The CTO report: per task class and capability class pair, cost and quality from the benchmark composer and the comparable-run count of D-005 against the recorded ten, labelled qualitative review below ten; tier distribution; every controller decision booked in the period with its readings, threshold, action, booking month and instant exactly as recorded, its stored statements printed verbatim as recorded text with their recording instant (R-011); each selection's basis and statement exactly as recorded, a configured rating never shown as observed quality; the re-verification line of D-007 for every week beginning before 2027-02-01 on the datastore's date. The CFO summary: company booked metered spend against the decided ceiling and the envelope, the standing charge separately as a recorded amount with no recorded home, never netted; per-channel spend and utilisation, unmeasured naming the missing amount where no budget amount is recorded; revenue, profit and every revenue-derived figure not yet earnable before programme entry or unmeasured, never a zero amount.   - M-015 and M-016: the measurable-now command takes its month from the reader; two commands, weekly and dashboard, are added; the delivered commands keep their names.
- Contract compatibility notes:
  - M-001 transition strategy. Current shape: seven ordered additive resources. Target shape: an eighth appended. Compatibility approach: every object is created only where absent, the initial register entries are inserted only where absent, and the widening keeps every stored value (A-010). Coexistence period: none. Retirement condition: none. Rollback position: the eighth resource's tables are dropped in reverse order, lossless while empty apart from the initial register entries, which the resource re-creates; the widened columns are restored to their bound, which succeeds while no stored value exceeds it.
  - M-002 transition strategy. Current shape: no company record reader. Target shape: a reader port with read members only. Compatibility approach: additive; the read-only member assertion names the new port's members. Coexistence period: the delivered readers stay for their delivered callers. Retirement condition: none in this change. Rollback position: removing the port with its adapter and the two commands.
  - M-014 transition strategy. Current shape: two shapes. Target shape: three, the third for non-money recorded figures. Compatibility approach: a new sealed shape with its own factory and rendering; existing constructions and renderings unchanged; the membership assertion names it. Coexistence period: none. Retirement condition: none. Rollback position: reverting the type with the management read models that use it.
- Schema or migration changes:
  - The register table is created with its write-once and supersession trigger and its initial entries inserted where absent; direction forward; reversible by dropping it. Readers during transition: before the resource exists the read ends naming the missing resource.
  - The policy statement register and the re-verification result record are created empty with write-once and datastore-stamp checks; direction forward; reversible by dropping them while empty.
  - The held-outcome record is created empty, write-once and checked at or above the horizon under the shared hold like every other admission row (F-006); direction forward; reversible by dropping it while empty. Writers during transition: the gateway writes it only once the change is deployed; earlier held rows have none.
  - The readings table's amount, spend and utilisation columns lose their precision bound; direction forward; reversible by restoring the bound while no value exceeds it; every stored value and the write-once rule are unchanged.
  - No data migration over existing rows arises: no row is copied, moved, rewritten or deleted.

## Operational Considerations

- Logging and observability updates: each report prints its instant, stored horizon, period, finality and the named outcome of a failed read, and the dashboard prints the same header (M-003, M-016); no new log sink is added.
- Error handling strategy: the read ends in exactly one of three named outcomes, unavailable while the store is being changed naming the bound reached, the eighth resource missing, or failed naming the datastore class; a rule over an unmeasured reading abstains rather than failing (M-003, M-011, C-002, C-012). The held-outcome write shares the admission transaction, so a held outcome and its record commit or roll back together (M-006). The widened columns remove the uncaught overflow (F-012).
- Security considerations: the management paths are read-only by construction, a read-only transaction refusing any write the datastore receives, and the boundary assertions hold the composers and rules free of ports with write members, capability references and network types (C-003, C-007, C-009, M-019); the dashboard prints composed lines only, never the connection string, a handle or a credential (C-009); register entries change no control because nothing reads them except the composers (C-011); no new external exposure is added, so no threat review is opened.
- Performance considerations: the read is one transaction of bounded statements over a store whose period volumes are a few items a week (C-002, M-003); the lock and statement bounds keep it inside the delivered 30-second command timeout whatever any other transaction holds; no index is added because no measured bottleneck exists.
- Deployment or operability impact: the eighth resource applies on the next install of a demonstration store; the company store stays at zero tables (C-017, M-001); the weekly and dashboard commands are run by the operator on request.
