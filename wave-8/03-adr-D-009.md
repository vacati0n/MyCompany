# Architecture Decision Record D-009

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-009
- Title: A closed catalogue of seven anchored recommendation rules, each abstaining over an unmeasured reading and evaluated in catalogue order
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-011, T-026 and T-042; sequencing constraints P-008

## Context

- Problem statement: recommendations must come from fixed labelled rules, and no COO threshold is recorded anywhere.
- Business and technical constraints: C-012 closed anchored rules with abstention; C-008 no invented threshold; C-007 no model call.
- Current architecture baseline: F-014 records the fixed controller thresholds; F-029 the owner's decisions and open questions; F-011 the recorded escalation of a held request; F-021 the awaiting-approval figure.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: M-011 holds seven rules, CFO rule 1 company ceiling, CFO rule 2 unbudgeted channel, CTO rule 1 qualitative review, CTO rule 2 controller action, CTO rule 3 re-verification, COO rule 1 escalated deferral and COO rule 2 awaiting approval, each keyed on a recorded anchor or measurement case, each output naming its rule, reading, case and anchor or abstaining naming its reading, evaluated once per read in catalogue order; CTO outputs carry the ten fields of section 33.
- Scope of impact: M-011 evaluates; M-012 carries outputs; M-018 names the evaluation.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009 and O-010, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record; a rule on a clean-record or approval-workload threshold was not considered because C-008 forbids it

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: every recommendation is traceable to a recorded anchor and none rests on an unmeasured reading.
- Tradeoffs accepted: most rules abstain or stay silent over today's records; the COO anchors are the request's own recorded escalation and measurement case, not an owner threshold.
- Risks introduced: R-001, a rule reading outside the composed lines.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of recommendations resting on an unmeasured reading; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of differences between two evaluations over one read; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Each rule fired and starved in turn over fixtures.
  - Review of each anchor against the recorded thresholds and decisions.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed for a rule if its anchor is found unrecorded; the rule is withdrawn from the catalogue.

- Tech Lead: omn-tech-lead at the Design Gate; unsigned
- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): omn-product-owner for the recommendation field count; unsigned
