# Architecture Decision Record D-005

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-005
- Title: One comparable-run count keys both selection's evidence threshold of the owner's ten and the CTO report's review label
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-005, T-022, T-031 and T-047; sequencing constraints P-005 and P-007

## Context

- Problem statement: delivered selection applies no observation minimum, the owner has recorded ten comparable runs per task, and no unit of a comparable run is defined.
- Business and technical constraints: C-013 the owner's ten; C-008 no invented quantity; C-012 the CTO label rests on the count.
- Current architecture baseline: F-015 records the delivered evidence rule and its four checks; F-016 the observation's facts and the absent task-to-capability relation; F-029 the owner's decision.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: one comparable run is one observation of a task class on a route with quality observed in the rating unit and its operation's cost stated; a capability class and task class pair counts the least over its admitted routes; selection ranks on evidence only at ten or more for every candidate with the delivered rule holding, and states every count; the CTO label is qualitative below ten; the ten is a recorded figure naming the owner's decision.
- Scope of impact: M-009 applies it; M-010 counts with the same pure function; M-013 records the ten; M-020 re-points the four checks.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-009 and O-010, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record

2. O-008, the ten as a datastore register row
- Benefits: the figure editable by a record
- Risks: a further table for a constant the owner decided once
- Why not selected: not selected on reuse leverage

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: the report and the router cannot disagree on whether a task is below ten.
- Tradeoffs accepted: delivered evidence checks are re-pointed to ten observations.
- Risks introduced: R-007, re-pointed checks; R-012, two counts.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of evidence rankings with any candidate below ten; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of tasks labelled evidence with a count below ten; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Selection and the label at nine and at ten comparable runs over fixtures.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the owner records another unit or figure; only the count function and the recorded figure change.

- Tech Lead: omn-tech-lead at the Design Gate; unsigned
- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): the owner's ten is the owner's recorded decision; unsigned
