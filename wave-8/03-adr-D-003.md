# Architecture Decision Record D-003

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-003
- Title: The four cases render through the delivered renderings, and the recorded-amount type gains a recorded-figure shape for recorded counts, percentages and dates
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-003, T-018 and T-036; sequencing constraints P-002 and P-012

## Context

- Problem statement: the owner's ten, the controller thresholds, the programme thresholds and the dated changes are recorded figures that are not money, and the recorded-amount type carries money only.
- Business and technical constraints: C-005 the union stays at three cases and the four render apart; C-008 no invented quantity; C-015 assertions never weakened.
- Current architecture baseline: F-017 records the union, the recorded-amount type and their renderings; F-025 the membership assertion; F-018 the envelope constants.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: every figure is a measurement quantity or a recorded amount rendered by its own delivered rendering; a third recorded shape carries a decimal or a date, its unit and where it was recorded, created by its own factory and rendered as recorded; every surface renders a line through one line rendering.
- Scope of impact: M-014 gains the shape; M-012 carries it; M-019 names it; M-023 is unchanged.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009 and O-010, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record; a fourth measurement case was not considered because C-005 forbids it

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: no recorded figure can render as observed, and a tile cannot render a line differently.
- Tradeoffs accepted: the recorded-amount type has three shapes.
- Risks introduced: R-001, a value composed outside the line rendering.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of recorded figures rendered as observed; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of unmeasured renderings carrying an amount, a unit or a zero; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Side-by-side rendering of every case on the report and the dashboard.
  - The membership assertion over the new shape.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the recorded-figure shape is found to admit a value without its source; the shape is then withdrawn and the figures carried as recorded money-free labels.

- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): not scope-impacting; unsigned
