# Architecture Decision Record D-006

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-006
- Title: A write-once held-outcome record written in the admission transaction makes every deferred request visible with its reason, hold age and escalation state
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-004, T-019 and T-037; sequencing constraints P-001 and P-006

## Context

- Problem statement: a deferral records its reason only in free text and a threshold deferral records no escalation instant, so the COO report cannot state reason or escalation state from a record.
- Business and technical constraints: C-014 every deferral listed; C-016 additive change; C-015 the horizon unweakened.
- Current architecture baseline: F-011 records the delivered deferral rows; F-006 the horizon discipline; F-012 the write-once decision record.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the eighth resource adds a write-once, horizon-checked record of every held outcome, carrying its operation, closed reason, escalation instant, hold timeout and escalation target, written by the ledger in the admission transaction from what the gateway passes; the COO report lists every deferral from it with its hold age and escalation state on the report instant.
- Scope of impact: M-001 creates it; M-006 writes it; M-007 passes it; M-010 reads it through M-003.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-010, reading the free-text failure reason
- Benefits: no new record
- Risks: no escalation instant for a threshold deferral and prose parsed at report time
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-014

2. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008 and O-009, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: every deferral is listed from a record, with its own recorded escalation instant.
- Tradeoffs accepted: held rows recorded before the resource read their escalation not recorded.
- Risks introduced: R-006, earlier held rows.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of deferrals without reason, hold age or escalation state on the COO report; the expected value is zero for rows recorded after the change.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - A deferral at threshold and one while an admission is in progress, listed from the record in a demonstration store.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed by dropping the record while empty; the COO deferral lines then read unmeasured naming the missing record.

- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): not scope-impacting; unsigned
