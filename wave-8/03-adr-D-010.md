# Architecture Decision Record D-010

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-010
- Title: The open-decisions register is a write-once datastore record with supersession, seeded once by the eighth resource and read inside the one snapshot
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-012, T-027 and T-043; sequencing constraints P-001 and P-009

## Context

- Problem statement: open owner decisions exist only in prose records, and the brief must list them from a recorded source in the same read.
- Business and technical constraints: C-011 write-once with supersession, changing no control; C-001 in the snapshot; C-017 the company store untouched.
- Current architecture baseline: F-029 records the decision record's entries and open questions; F-027 the master plan's decision entity; F-026 the installer; F-012 the write-once refusal.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the eighth resource creates the register, each entry carrying identifier, statement, kind, status, owner, recorded date, interim ruling, decider and decision date, superseded entry and datastore entry instant, a trigger refusing update, delete and any supersession of no existing entry; it inserts the initial transcription where absent; no other writer is added; the brief's decisions-required section is its open entries in the snapshot.
- Scope of impact: M-001 creates and seeds it; M-003 reads it; M-010 composes the section.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-005, the register as a repository file
- Benefits: no datastore change
- Risks: outside the snapshot and editable in place
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-001

2. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009 and O-010, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: open decisions come from a record nobody can edit in place.
- Tradeoffs accepted: later entries need a further resource or a directed writer.
- Risks introduced: R-009, the register lagging; R-015, a mis-transcription.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of open questions of the two records absent from the register; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of controls changed by recording an entry; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Entry-by-entry comparison with the two records.
  - An in-place edit refused in a demonstration store.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the owner directs another home for the register; the entries move by a resource, never by edit.

- Architect: producing agent; does not sign
- Tech Lead: omn-tech-lead at the Design Gate; unsigned
- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): omn-product-owner for the recording of later entries; unsigned
