# Architecture Decision Record D-007

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-007
- Title: A policy statement register and a write-once re-verification result record hold the re-verification status, with no writer in this wave and the line unmeasured until results are recorded
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-007, T-021 and T-038; sequencing constraints P-001 and P-007

## Context

- Problem statement: the CTO report must show which policy statements were re-checked, changed and overdue before 2027-02-01, and no record holds statements or results.
- Business and technical constraints: C-001 the record is in the snapshot; C-008 no invented cadence; C-011 a record changes no control.
- Current architecture baseline: F-024 records no policy representation; F-027 the master plan's platform-policy entity; F-028 the monthly re-verification wording; F-032 the dated delta held in prose.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the eighth resource adds a statement register shaped after the platform-policy entity and a write-once result record of re-verifications stamped by the datastore; the line reads re-checked and changed statements from results of the week, unmeasured naming the missing record where none is recorded, and overdue unmeasured naming the missing cadence until the owner confirms one.
- Scope of impact: M-001 creates both; M-003 reads them; M-010 composes the line.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-005, the record as a repository file
- Benefits: no datastore change
- Risks: outside the snapshot and editable in place
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-001

2. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009 and O-010, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: the line is honest today and fills when results are recorded.
- Tradeoffs accepted: the line reads unmeasured in every store until a writer or transcription is directed.
- Risks introduced: R-018, no cadence or writer confirmed.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of re-verification lines stating a status no result records; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Weeks either side of 2027-02-01 on the datastore's date, with and without a fixture result.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the owner directs the master plan's entity to live elsewhere; the line then reads from that record.

- Architect: producing agent; does not sign
- Tech Lead: omn-tech-lead at the Design Gate; unsigned
- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): the writer and cadence are the owner's; unsigned
