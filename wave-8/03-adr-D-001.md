# Architecture Decision Record D-001

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-001
- Title: The report set reads every source in one read-only repeatable-read snapshot that fixes its instant in its first statement, reads the record horizon as stored, and never closes, holds or waits
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-9; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0009; planner tasks T-001, T-017 and T-035; sequencing constraints P-003, P-004 and P-012

## Context

- Problem statement: the delivered readings each take their own transaction at their own instant, the delivered month reads close the horizon, a write, and read at the default isolation, so a report built from them shows several instants, writes, and can wait on a hold an admission keeps across a provider call.
- Business and technical constraints: C-001 one snapshot at one instant; C-002 no wait on any hold of any duration, inside the read's own timeout, with a named outcome; C-003 no write and no horizon advance; C-004 the datastore's clock.
- Current architecture baseline: F-004 and F-008 record the delivered transactions and isolation; F-005 the closure and its stored-horizon fallback; F-006 the horizon discipline; F-007 the finality rule; F-009 the absence of any lock or statement bound and the 30, 120 and unbounded command timeouts; F-010 the admission held across the provider call.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: M-003 opens one repeatable-read, read-only transaction on the data source; it sets transaction-local lock and statement bounds inside the 30-second command timeout; its first statement reads the datastore's statement-start clock as the report instant, the horizon as stored, the earliest audit entry and the period bounds; every source is read by later statements of the same transaction, none taking a row hold, an advisory hold or the closure; finality is the stored horizon against the period's end; the read ends in a named outcome when a bound is reached, the eighth resource is missing, or the datastore fails.
- Scope of impact: M-002 declares the port; M-003 implements it; M-004 and M-005 make the delivered statements callable on the snapshot; M-027 is untouched.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-001, composing the delivered readings each in its own transaction
- Benefits: no new reader
- Risks: lines at several instants and several closures per report
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-001

2. O-003, closing the month first inside the read
- Benefits: finality made by every report
- Risks: a write on the read path and admissions waiting on the exclusive hold
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-003 and C-002

3. O-004, a serializable read-only deferrable snapshot
- Benefits: a serialisable snapshot
- Risks: the read waits for a safe snapshot
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-002

4. O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009 and O-010, the other options of the package evaluation
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: every line, tile and rule output carries one instant from one snapshot, and no hold of any length reaches the read.
- Tradeoffs accepted: a week reads not final until a delivered closure runs after it ends; a structural change of the store ends the read rather than waiting.
- Risks introduced: R-001, a value read outside the snapshot; R-002, weeks reading not final; R-004, a structural hold ending the read; R-019, the statement bound on a slow host.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of distinct instants across the lines of one report; the expected value is one.
  - The count of writes and of horizon advances made by a report; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of reads exceeding the command timeout or ending in an unnamed failure; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - A commit of an operation, a gate transition and an alert during composition, reaching every affected line or none.
  - A read during an admission held across a stand-in provider call, and against holds kept past the read's timeout.
  - A week stated final, re-read identical after a later commit.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if a datastore wait or conflict reaches the read despite A-004; the read then takes its sources with the same instant through a closure-free fallback per statement group, recorded as a new decision.
