- Title: The produce command's modes and named refusals
- Date: at emission in run-7ae81c0de400
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-10; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: No production command exists.
- Business and technical constraints: C-003, C-005, C-009, C-016.
- Current architecture baseline: F-015.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: Fake, plan-only and metered modes; the plan, estimate, cap and bounds printed first; metered refusals naming each unmet condition and each missing variable exactly.
- Scope of impact: M-018, M-019.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-002: Production types in the host executable
- Benefits: No new assembly
- Risks: Production path testable only through the executable; host no longer composition only
- Why not selected: Ties on hard constraints, impact and reuse; loses on quality

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: The owner sees what will be called before any call.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Exit codes are a new contract.
- Risks introduced: R-019

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Output order; refusals per condition.
- Verification checkpoints: Mode tests in P-011.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A call observed before the plan prints reverses the ordering.
