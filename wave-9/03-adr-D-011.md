## Decision

- Decision statement: A reviewable file quoting every beat, graphic, clip and thumbnail entry from its source, checked by the loader; alternative text only where recorded.
- Scope of impact: M-024, M-012.

- Tradeoffs accepted: Review of the material is a delivery step.
- Risks introduced: R-014, R-021
