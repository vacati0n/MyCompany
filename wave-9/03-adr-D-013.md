# Architecture Decision Record D-013

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-013
- Title: One process starter for renderer and probe
- Date: at emission in run-7ae81c0de400
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-10; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: Rendering, probing, decoding and drawing each start the external tool.
- Business and technical constraints: C-007, C-008, C-016.
- Current architecture baseline: F-040, F-052.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: ExternalMediaTool alone starts the renderer and probe from settings, with argument lists, text through files, staging under the output root, bounds and the version line recorded.
- Scope of impact: M-020.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-007: Stills drawn in process by a new package
- Benefits: No process for stills
- Risks: A new dependency and a second drawing engine
- Why not selected: Loses on impact, one dependency-change module more

2. O-009: Tracked tree found by version control
- Benefits: Exact tracked-tree test
- Risks: A second process-starting site
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-007

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: One reviewed process site.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Stills depend on the installed tool's determinism.
- Risks introduced: R-009, R-018

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Process-starter scan; bound endings.
- Verification checkpoints: Assertion with a probe and stalled stand-in tool in P-008.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A second process site found reverses the boundary.

- Architect: unsigned; the producer does not accept its own record
- Tech Lead: unsigned; acceptance belongs to the Design Gate
