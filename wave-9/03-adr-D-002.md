# Architecture Decision Record D-002

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-002
- Title: Internal vendor adapters with body-read usage and per-account scheme
- Date: at emission in run-7ae81c0de400
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-10; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: The only adapter invents its body and usage headers and books failures at zero; the exchange knows only one scheme; the boundary always uses channel-scoped names.
- Business and technical constraints: C-001, C-005, C-010.
- Current architecture baseline: F-003, F-010, F-011, F-013.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: Three internal sealed adapters for the speech, image and messages paths read usage from the body and mark the charge unknown on timeout, transport failure, non-success or missing usage; accounts record scope and scheme; company-level accounts use the GLOBAL name.
- Scope of impact: M-006, M-007, M-008.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-002: Production types in the host executable
- Benefits: No new assembly
- Risks: Production path testable only through the executable; host no longer composition only
- Why not selected: Ties on hard constraints, impact and reuse; loses on quality

2. O-005: Fakes built by the metered factory for reserved endpoint values
- Benefits: One factory
- Risks: A fake is constructible in a metered composition
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-003

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: Vendor shapes are spoken; no attempt is booked at zero when it may have been charged; names are knowable before any channel row.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Every narration booking carries the estimate basis while the body has no usage.
- Risks introduced: R-001, R-007, R-011

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Fixture source and date per path; fake-secret search hits.
- Verification checkpoints: Contract tests in P-006; first-hand verification before the go.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A first-hand verification contradicting a fixture reverses the affected path.
