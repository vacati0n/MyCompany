# Architecture Decision Record D-005

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-005
- Title: A write-once store designation and a host guard per mode
- Date: at emission in run-7ae81c0de400
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-10; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: Nothing distinguishes a demonstration store from the company store.
- Business and technical constraints: C-005, C-009, C-011.
- Current architecture baseline: F-016, F-017, F-018.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: A one-row designation record; a pure guard admits fake mode on demonstration and metered mode on company with matching identity; every reader labels demonstration figures.
- Scope of impact: M-012, M-015, M-016, M-018.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-002: Production types in the host executable
- Benefits: No new assembly
- Risks: Production path testable only through the executable; host no longer composition only
- Why not selected: Ties on hard constraints, impact and reuse; loses on quality

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: A fake can never be booked as an observation and the company store is never a test target.
- Tradeoffs accepted: An undesignated store is refused by both modes.
- Risks introduced: R-022

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Refusals per designation; labels on every line.
- Verification checkpoints: Guard tests over values and service tests in demonstration stores in P-011.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A store designated wrongly reverses the guard's trust in the record.

## Approval

- Architect: unsigned; the producer does not accept its own record
- Tech Lead: unsigned; acceptance belongs to the Design Gate
- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): unsigned
