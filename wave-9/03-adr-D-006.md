# Architecture Decision Record D-006

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-006
- Title: A recorded item cap with a durable worst-case reservation
- Date: at emission in run-7ae81c0de400
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-10; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: The cap must count every possibly charged attempt, including lost and unrecorded ones, before any later admission.
- Business and technical constraints: C-001, C-002, C-008.
- Current architecture baseline: F-004, F-005, F-006, F-023, F-024, F-025.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: The admission snapshot reads cap, counted total and open reservations; resolution refuses a worst case past the remaining cap; a capped provider admission commits its reservation on a companion transaction before the call; the booking reconciles it; unknown charges book at worst case.
- Scope of impact: M-005, M-009, M-010, M-013, M-016.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-003: Cap held by scope holds only
- Benefits: No reservation table
- Risks: A lost or unrecorded attempt is invisible to the next admission
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-001

2. O-004: No transaction during the call; two instants
- Benefits: Shorter holds
- Risks: Drops the one reserved booking instant
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-002

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: The cap is never passed by a loss, a failed recording or a concurrent run.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Uncapped admissions keep the delivered path; an unreconciled reservation counts at worst case.
- Risks introduced: R-003, R-004, R-005, R-006

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Counted total against the cap per attempt kind and for a concurrent pair.
- Verification checkpoints: Stub-priced runs binding mid-production in P-006 and P-011.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A reservation found invisible to a concurrent admission reverses the decision.
