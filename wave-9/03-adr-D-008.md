# Architecture Decision Record D-008

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-008
- Title: Every timeout, hold and clock enumerated, with bounds for ruling
- Date: at emission in run-7ae81c0de400
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-10; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: The render, probe and decode have no bound and records are stamped by two clocks.
- Business and technical constraints: C-008, C-002.
- Current architecture baseline: F-007, F-008, F-009, F-012, F-049, F-050.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: The enumeration of section 5.3 of the design; the call bound unchanged with a narration character maximum; render and decode 60 minutes, probe and still 60 seconds proposed; every recorded instant on the datastore.
- Scope of impact: M-005, M-020.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-004: No transaction during the call; two instants
- Benefits: Shorter holds
- Risks: Drops the one reserved booking instant
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-002

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: Every wait ends named and no hold spans a process run.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Proposals, not measurements, until ruled.
- Risks introduced: R-002, R-017

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Bound endings under stalled stand-ins; open transactions during a render.
- Verification checkpoints: Stalled provider, stalled tool and skewed process clock tests in P-010.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A ruling changing a bound is applied by setting, not code.
