# Architecture Decision Record D-004

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-004
- Title: Fakes only in a demonstration composition
- Date: at emission in run-7ae81c0de400
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-10; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: Fakes must share the vendor contracts yet never reach the network or a metered run.
- Business and technical constraints: C-003, C-005, C-014.
- Current architecture baseline: F-014, F-028, F-054.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: A demonstration factory member builds fakes, a placeholder secret store and no network client; the metered member cannot construct a fake.
- Scope of impact: M-006, M-007.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-005: Fakes built by the metered factory for reserved endpoint values
- Benefits: One factory
- Risks: A fake is constructible in a metered composition
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-003

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: Fake mode is network-free by construction and reads no credential variable.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Fakes reuse the real route rows in demonstration stores.
- Risks introduced: R-022

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Network requests in fake mode; fake calls after a metered failure.
- Verification checkpoints: A failing-on-network handler in P-010.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A fake found reachable from the metered member reverses the decision.

- Architect: unsigned; the producer does not accept its own record
- Tech Lead: unsigned; acceptance belongs to the Design Gate
- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): unsigned
