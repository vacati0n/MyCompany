## Business Context

- Problem statement: the company has never produced a video; item 001 is scripted, attributed and recorded but held at Production, Audio and Copyright check, and today no part of the company's software can turn a recorded package into a video: a call to an outside service returns an operation record and no content, only one of the twelve stages has a handler and none produces media, nothing can be rendered or stored, unit kinds other than input, output and cached units are never costed, and nothing holds the spend of one video to an owner-set cap, so the owner's authority to spend up to USD 5.95 on a first video cannot be exercised safely.
- Value hypothesis: if one command produces the video from the recorded package, proven end to end at zero spend with fake providers whose contracts are identical to the real vendors', and the metered mode refuses before any call unless every precondition is met and stops at the cap, then the owner can release the single metered run with a known plan, a known worst-case cost and no route to publication, and the first real production records will populate the delivered brief and dashboard with no further build.
- Affected users: the CEO and sole owner, who authorised the spend, sets the credentials and gives the go; the orchestrator, who executes the single metered run outside the phases; the operator roles that run the company's entry point; and every later item, which inherits a re-pointable production path.
- Success measure: in the demonstration store a fake-mode run of the produce command ends with one decodable video file under the output root, its runtime measured from the file, twelve stage outcomes and one operation record per call, and USD 0.00 spend labelled demonstration; the metered mode, with no credentials set, refuses before any call and names exactly the variables the owner documentation lists; the printed plan states an estimated total at or below the cap; and no phase reaches a real endpoint.
