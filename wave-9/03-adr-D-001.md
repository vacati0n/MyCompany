# Architecture Decision Record D-001

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-001
- Title: The completed outcome carries the produced content
- Date: at emission in run-7ae81c0de400
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-10; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: No outcome hands back what a call produced, so no stage can obtain narration or an image.
- Business and technical constraints: C-002, C-010, C-016.
- Current architecture baseline: F-001, F-002.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: Completed gains an optional content member holding kind, media type, one read-only buffer, its length and its SHA-256 hash; ProviderAttempt carries the same; only Completed can hold content.
- Scope of impact: M-005, M-006.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-002: Production types in the host executable
- Benefits: No new assembly
- Risks: Production path testable only through the executable; host no longer composition only
- Why not selected: Ties on hard constraints, impact and reuse; loses on quality

2. O-003: Cap held by scope holds only
- Benefits: No reservation table
- Risks: A lost or unrecorded attempt is invisible to the next admission
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-001

3. O-005: Fakes built by the metered factory for reserved endpoint values
- Benefits: One factory
- Risks: A fake is constructible in a metered composition
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-003

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: Stages receive content through the one boundary; length and hash are over the bytes received.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Content is buffered in memory once per call.
- Risks introduced: R-011

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Content length and hash against received bytes; non-completed outcomes carrying none.
- Verification checkpoints: Contract tests against fakes and the stub handler in P-006.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: Content lost or duplicated between adapter and caller reverses the decision.
