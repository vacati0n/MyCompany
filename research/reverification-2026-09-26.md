### 4.1 Became establishable this pass

**[OFFICIAL] US tax withholding on AdSense for a Vietnam-resident payee** — previously policy dossier §7.3 **#12**, described there as *"Material and unresolved."* It is now establishable from two first-party sources, and the answer is adverse:

- **[OFFICIAL POLICY]** YouTube publishes the mechanics: *"If you submit U.S. tax info, tax withholding rates are between 0-30% on earnings you get from U.S. viewers"*; for a non-US payee with no treaty benefit *"the default withholding rate will be 30% of U.S. earnings"*; and if no tax info is provided at all, up to *"24% of your total earnings worldwide."* Source: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10391362>
- **[OFFICIAL]** The IRS's own A-to-Z list of United States income tax treaties **does not include Vietnam** (under V it lists Venezuela only). Source: <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z>

**[INFERENCE]** Read together: a Vietnam-resident payee who correctly submits US tax info has **no treaty rate available** and faces the **30% default on US-viewer-sourced earnings**. *This is reasoning from the two quoted first-party sources; neither states the combination, and neither Google nor the IRS publishes a Vietnam-specific YouTube determination.* For an **English-language** channel this is not a marginal detail — US viewership is where the higher RPMs sit, and 30% comes off precisely that share.

**Why this does not change the committed plan:** the $77.41 envelope is a **cost** figure and no revenue forecast is committed against it. ## 5. Method and limitations

- **First-party only.** Every statement labelled [OFFICIAL] or [OFFICIAL POLICY] above carries the vendor's or platform's own URL. Two [INFERENCE] statements are made (the Vietnam withholding combination in §4.1, and the Storyblocks/Uppbeat non-equivalence in X-5) and both are marked as reasoning, with the quoted sources they reason from.
- **JS-rendered pages.** TikTok's Community Guidelines, Storyblocks, Uppbeat, Epidemic Sound and Artlist return navigation chrome only to an ordinary fetcher. TikTok, Storyblocks, Uppbeat and Epidemic Sound were recovered by rendering in a browser and extracting `innerText`; quotes are verbatim from that render. **Artlist could not be recovered by either method** and is recorded as unobtained, not as unchanged. Meta's Business Help Centre could not be recovered this pass at all.
- **Summariser paraphrase.** One fetch returned a paraphrase of the YouTube reused-content bullets that differed from the dossier's quotes. Rather than report a change, the page was re-fetched with an exact-quote instruction; **all four dossier bullets were present word-for-word**, and the page's own update log ends at July 2025. Item 8 records this as a dossier quoting subset, not a change. **This is the discipline the brief asked for: a paraphrase difference is not a finding.**
- **Month-granularity logs.** YouTube's ad-guideline log dates entries by month only. Two September 2026 entries therefore cannot be placed relative to 18 Sep, and X-2 says so rather than guessing.
- **Negative findings remain weaker evidence.** "No changelog entry after August 2026" is an absence and can be defeated by one page not located. It is corroborated here by the unchanged verbatim text of five separate YouTube policy pages, but it is still an absence.
- **Scope discipline.** This pass re-checked only what the brief named plus anything a first-party source showed as dated in the 18–26 Sep window. It is **not** a fresh survey. Meta and Instagram were not re-surveyed; TikTok was checked only for the four items above.
