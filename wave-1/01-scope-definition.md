## Metadata

- Feature name: Wave 1: Company Foundation
- Requested by: the CEO and sole owner, through the supplied feature request `tasks/MC-2/input.md`
- Business goal: the company can account for what it spends, state why it was permitted to use what it publishes, and keep reserved authority where it belongs, from its own record rather than from assertion
- Target outcome: one channel's operation is fully accounted for — every AI operation attributed and rolled up against the approved monthly envelope, every asset's permission basis answerable from the record alone, every reserved approval recorded with the time it cost, and no capacity obtained by means a provider's terms forbid
- Scope decision date: 2026-09-26

## Business Context

- Problem statement: the company holds no operating record, so it cannot say what an operation cost and against which item, why it was permitted to use an asset, who approved a release and how long that approval took, or which capacity source served a task — and without that record the cost envelope, the rights position, the separation of duties and the approval baseline that the earning model depends on cannot be held or evidenced.
- Value hypothesis: if every operation, permission basis, approval and cost is recorded as it happens, the company can prove its cost and rights position from its own record, and can later relax the owner's per-release approval on measured evidence rather than on assumption — which is the constraint that decides how many channels one owner can run.
- Affected users: the CEO and owner, as the mandatory approver of every publication and the weekly reviewer; the workforce roles that produce, verify, clear and account for content; the finance, copyright, policy and publishing responsibilities that later read this record; any party that must be shown the basis on which an asset was used.
- Success measure: cost per item and the monthly total are computable from the record and comparable against the approved envelope of USD 77.41 per month; the permission basis of every asset is answerable from the record alone; the three forbidden privilege acts are each refused and the refusal recorded; minutes per owner approval are recorded from the first approval; and every measure that needs a revenue parameter is reported as not yet available rather than as a number.

## Constraints and Dependencies

- Business constraints: the approved monthly envelope is USD 77.41 for the first channel, 34.42 metered and 42.99 standing, and every unit price is re-fetched immediately before any spend; one English-language channel on one publishing platform, one complete video then three a week, then expansion; revenue at launch is platform advertising plus affiliate links; visual sourcing is licensed stock and music with original motion graphics and sparing short generated cutaways; the CEO approves every publication individually, and that step relaxes only by an explicit recorded CEO decision; the nine-wave plan is kept in full, with `RK-014` accepted and its mitigation declined.
