## Recommendation

- Recommended option: O-002
- Rationale: O-002 is the only option that meets every must-have criterion. It meets `EC-001` on computed capacity obtained entirely through commercial interfaces, `EC-002` because every figure in it divides out of the ledger recorded above, `EC-003` because each capability carries a named secondary and a degraded mode, `EC-004` because it funds an L3 fact-check and a separate L3 compliance gate pack against a regime whose penalties reach the channel, and `EC-005` because it holds a commercial grant and a registration obligation for every asset class.
