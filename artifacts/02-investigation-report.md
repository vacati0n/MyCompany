## Recommendation

- Recommended option: `O-001`
- Rationale: the evidence favours carrying this base forward as it stands rather than holding for first-party re-verification or accepting it as settled. The framing's blocking question on current platform policy is answered for all four platforms in scope, and the answer is specific enough to bind an architecture: the binding constraint is not that generation is used but that templated, minimally-transformed output is caught by name on every platform, through the templated-mass-production prohibition, the unoriginal and low-quality regime, the non-transformative list that includes plain narration and background music and that is stated to apply to licensed footage as well, and the prohibited formats that exclude slideshows, static video and text montages outright. The split that matters most for an automated pipeline is established from quoted rules rather than inferred: the fatal enforcement is at channel and account level, being termination of the account and any associated channels on a third copyright strike, an outright channel-level monetization bar for synthetic personas on health, legal, finance or political topics, permanent programme ineligibility at five account violations plus feed suppression with no violation at all, an extendable ninety-day account demonetization that can withhold accrued earnings, and removal from all recommendation surfaces at ten unoriginal reposts in thirty days, while the recoverable failures are video-level and carry one appeal each. The 2027 change is established with its grandfathering scope corrected: entry thresholds rise for new entrants to a thousand subscribers with eight thousand watch hours or twenty million short-form views, this company has not published and is therefore a new entrant, and the separate continuously re-tested ten-million short-form floor and the new activity requirement apply to everyone rather than only to new entrants. The economics are equally decisive and are recorded as the estimates their source declares them to be: research, scripting, fact-checking, thumbnails and narration together are about one and a half dollars for a ten-minute item, while generating the pictures is thirty to two hundred and forty dollars for the same item and between twelve and a hundred times everything else for a short, so the visual-sourcing choice rather than the language-model choice sets the budget the CEO deferred. The terms position bears directly on the supplied account-pool design: consumer subscriptions are foreclosed as production capacity by two providers explicitly and by the third in practice, no provider publishes a one-account rule while three separately prohibit the circumvention that multi-accounting for capacity would amount to, and the consumer video allowance is about eighty seconds a month. Holding the gate is not favoured because no role in this run is established as able to reach external sources and the re-verification the dossiers ask for is recurring rather than a single act; accepting the base as settled is not favoured because both dossiers record what they could not establish and the framing and the intent both forbid treating unverified policy as fact. The reading is therefore that the base is sufficient to decide on, provided nothing in it is promoted above the confidence its source carries.
- Preconditions: that every statement drawn from the two dossiers travels at the label its source gave it, with no inference or industry-practice statement promoted to an observation and no cost figure presented as established cost; that the re-verification the policy evidence asks for is attached before the 24 September 2026 guidelines change and again before the 31 January 2027 terms deadline, and that the pricing evidence is re-fetched before any spend is committed; that the visual-sourcing question is settled before the bottom-up capacity computation the budget decision rests on is performed, because the same illustrative month costs about eighty-six or about five hundred and thirty-six dollars depending only on that; that the payee country of the operating entity is settled before channels are created, because one ad-payment account is permitted per payee name and three of the four platforms have no confirmed monetizable route for a Vietnam-resident payee; and that the launch-scope trade-off is returned to the CEO as the decision record itself directs.
- Risks requiring monitoring: channel-level and account-level enforcement, because the fatal thresholds are reached by accumulation and an automated pipeline produces correlated rather than independent failures, so publishing velocity is itself a risk variable; the two dated policy changes and the possibility that the corrected grandfathering reading is missed downstream; the account-pool design's dependence on capacity the providers' terms do not permit, together with the named prohibitions on multiple-account abuse and network circumvention on one publishing platform; the cost model's sensitivity to the single visual-sourcing decision; the unverified Vietnamese tax and regulatory position, which the evidence flags as material and directs to local counsel; the per-channel allowlisting of music and stock licences, where revenue lost before registration is stated to be unrecoverable; and the per-video CEO approval workload, which the decision record states grows linearly with channel count and which is not quantified anywhere for a four-platform, two-language launch.
