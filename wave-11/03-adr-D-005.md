# Architecture Decision Record D-005

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-005
- Title: Stills are one PNG; motion graphics are PNG frames with a manifest entered frame by frame into the delivered segment list, re-hashed and held before the encode
- Date: at emission in run-9d83536ecd16
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-12; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: The assembly joins stills through a segment list with one encode (F-006); motion must enter it with the hashed bytes being the bytes encoded and the boundaries unchanged.
- Business and technical constraints: C-003, C-004, C-011.
- Current architecture baseline: F-003, F-006, F-017.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: A still renders its final state once; a motion graphic renders exactly its segment's frames, moving over the style file's motion-end fraction of them and then holding; frames are promoted and listed in a manifest recorded as GraphicMotion; before RenderAsync the assembly re-hashes every still, manifest and frame, holds them until the encode exits, writes one entry per still and one per frame, and refuses if the cumulative duration rounding reaches half a frame.
- Scope of impact: M-001, M-004, M-005.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-008: The renderer's encoder writes a video per motion graphic
- Benefits: One file per graphic
- Risks: Mixed codecs in the segment list; a second lossy encode
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-011

2. O-009: The media tool encodes each motion graphic into a lossless intermediate
- Benefits: One file per graphic, the tech lead's stated preference
- Risks: The assembly reads an intermediate, not the hashed frames; one more reader pair
- Why not selected: Ties on constraints and impact; loses on reuse

3. O-015: Every frame its own artifact row
- Benefits: Per-frame rows
- Risks: About 6,180 more transactions with no added assurance
- Why not selected: Ties on constraints, impact and reuse; loses on operability

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: The encode reads exactly the bytes recorded; RenderAsync and the boundaries are unchanged.
- Tradeoffs accepted: A segment list of about 6,200 entries; motion encoded with the still-image tuning (Q-007).
- Risks introduced: R-010, R-011

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Files re-hashed before the encode; cumulative rounding per run; segment boundaries for fixed durations.
- Verification checkpoints: Motion through produce in P-010; the output round trip and the timeline regression in P-019.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: The encode reads a graphic file whose hash differs from its record, or a boundary moves for fixed durations.
