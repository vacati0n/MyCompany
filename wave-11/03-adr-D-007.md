# Architecture Decision Record D-007

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-007
- Title: A demonstration-only review command builds the sheet from byte copies of final frames and the preview from the production's own files
- Date: at emission in run-9d83536ecd16
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-12; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: The owner reviews all 22 graphics before any company-store run; a scaled tile cannot equal a full frame.
- Business and technical constraints: C-004, C-012, C-014.
- Current architecture baseline: F-006, F-033, F-034.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: review-graphics, refused on any designation but demonstration before any process, re-hashes each graphic output, copies each final frame byte for byte through the writer, composes one labelled sheet from those copies with the media tool, renders a video-only preview of the 22 graphics in position order at the production's recorded spans from its own stills and frames, checks one video stream and a zero-error decode, records ContactSheet, ContactSheetFrame and GraphicPreview, and prints every hash.
- Scope of impact: M-002, M-003, M-011, M-014.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-016: Claim tiles byte-identical to full frames
- Benefits: One file
- Risks: Cannot hold for a scaled tile
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-014

2. O-017: Draw the preview afresh as one composition
- Benefits: One render
- Risks: The preview's bytes are not the production's graphics
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-004

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: The owner approves the very bytes the production made, by hash.
- Tradeoffs accepted: One more command and three more roles; a version is reviewed once.
- Risks introduced: R-016

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Twenty-two final-frame copies equal by hash; preview stream count and decode errors.
- Verification checkpoints: The command in P-013; review evidence in P-019.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A tile's source copy differs from its graphic's final frame, or the command runs on a company store.

## Approval

- Architect: unsigned; the producer does not accept its own record
- Tech Lead: unsigned; acceptance belongs to the Design Gate
- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): unsigned
