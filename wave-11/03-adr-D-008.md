# Architecture Decision Record D-008

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-008
- Title: Every timeout, hold and clock enumerated; new bounds proposed for ruling, the render bound as a fixed plus per-frame allowance
- Date: at emission in run-9d83536ecd16
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-12; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: Graphics add a hashing pass, a bundle and 22 render processes to a path whose bounds are configured and ruled (F-011, F-012).
- Business and technical constraints: C-006, C-016.
- Current architecture baseline: F-001, F-012, F-017, F-021, F-024, F-026, F-036.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: Proposed for ruling: installation hashing 300 s; bundling 120 s against 8.3 s measured cold; per-graphic render 120 s plus 0.5 s a frame, applied to each render process; per-frame readiness 30 s; concurrency 1; the sheet borrows the still bound and the preview the render, probe and decode bounds; the datastore's 30 s command timeout holds no render; every bound on the monotonic clock and every instant the datastore's.
- Scope of impact: M-003, M-005, M-007, M-028.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-018: A single fixed per-graphic bound
- Benefits: One setting fewer
- Risks: A slower narration can push a long motion graphic past a bound sized on demonstration spans
- Why not selected: Ties on constraints, impact and reuse; loses on quality attributes

2. O-011: One bound for all graphics
- Benefits: Simplest
- Risks: No bound per graphic
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-006

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: Each graphic has its own bound that scales with its re-timed span; nothing is held while a process runs.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Two settings for one bound; every proposed value rests on the trial until measured.
- Risks introduced: R-006, R-022

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Observed seconds per graphic and per pass against each bound.
- Verification checkpoints: The ruling in P-001; measured times in P-018; every timeout's covering test in P-019.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A render passes its bound under normal load, or a render is observed inside a transaction.
