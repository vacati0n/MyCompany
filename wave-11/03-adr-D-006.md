# Architecture Decision Record D-006

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-006
- Title: Graphic provenance and sameness in an eleventh resource; render twice in demonstration; stills compared with the owner's approval file in the company store
- Date: at emission in run-9d83536ecd16
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-12; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: No graphic provenance exists and the role set is closed (F-009); sameness must be observed, and company stills must equal the approved bytes.
- Business and technical constraints: C-004, C-012, C-013, C-014.
- Current architecture baseline: F-007, F-009, F-013, F-037.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: db/011-graphics.sql widens the role check by four roles and adds a write-once, stamped graphic_provenance table keyed to the artifact; each graphic records its specification, style, program, bundle, renderer, browser, Node and face identities and hashes, its segment and changing frames, final-frame and frame-set digests, arguments, observed seconds and sameness; a demonstration store renders each graphic twice and compares; a company store renders once, compares each still with the approval file the orchestrator writes outside the store, and records motion as re-timed.
- Scope of impact: M-001, M-005, M-012, M-013.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-014: Record the owner's approval in the company store
- Benefits: Approval travels with the store
- Risks: A write to the company store before the go
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-012

2. O-015: Every frame its own artifact row
- Benefits: Per-frame rows
- Risks: About 6,180 more transactions
- Why not selected: Ties on constraints, impact and reuse; loses on operability

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: Every graphic reads back what drew it and whether it repeated; the company run cannot silently show an unapproved still.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Demonstration runs render everything twice; motion is never compared across runs.
- Risks introduced: R-007, R-014, R-019, R-020

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Provenance fields non-empty and equal to recomputation; sameness kinds per run.
- Verification checkpoints: Provenance and sameness in P-012; the resource applied twice over a company-shaped store in P-020.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A sameness statement is recorded without two compared renders, or a still differing from its approval is assembled.

- Tech Lead: unsigned; acceptance belongs to the Design Gate
- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): unsigned
