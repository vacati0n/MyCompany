# Architecture Decision Record D-004

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-004
- Title: Design executes after Audio from one timeline composed once; the Design stage stays a produce-service step
- Date: at emission in run-9d83536ecd16
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-12; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: Design runs before any beat duration is measured (F-005), while motion must be timed from the measured narration of the same run.
- Business and technical constraints: C-003, C-011, C-013, C-015, C-020.
- Current architecture baseline: F-005, F-006, F-008, F-014, F-029.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: After the carried stages the run executes Audio, composes the timeline once with BeatTimeline.Compose over the measured durations and planned paths, then Design, Thumbnail and Production; the assembly reuses that timeline; the Design stage is not an IStageHandler because its inputs are run-scoped.
- Scope of impact: M-005, M-010, M-019, M-024.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-005: Keep Design before Audio and time motion from the script
- Benefits: No order change
- Risks: Motion follows an expectation
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-003

2. O-006: Split Design into still and motion passes
- Benefits: Stills before narration
- Risks: Two outcomes for one stage need a changed key
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-013

3. O-007: Render motion inside the Production stage
- Benefits: Design keeps its place
- Risks: Provenance split; Design's record misses the twelve
- Why not selected: Ties on constraints, impact and reuse; loses on quality attributes

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: Every motion graphic and the assembly read one timeline; segment boundaries are identical by construction.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Three delivered live-test assertions follow the new order and roles (Q-002); a Design failure now follows a recorded narration.
- Risks introduced: R-008, R-017

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Stage stamps in the new order; motion windows inside segments for two stand-in narrations.
- Verification checkpoints: The first still through produce in P-009; timing evidence in P-019.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A motion graphic renders before the run's beat durations are measured.

- Tech Lead: unsigned; acceptance belongs to the Design Gate
