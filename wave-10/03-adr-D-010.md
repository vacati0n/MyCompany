# Architecture Decision Record D-010

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-010
- Title: The tenth resource, additive and write-once, applied alone to a store holding nine
- Date: at emission in run-dfcae1756c32
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-11; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: The store records two modes and no registration, measurement or provenance; a full install cannot be repeated over a populated store.
- Business and technical constraints: C-006, C-010, C-012.
- Current architecture baseline: F-011, F-012, F-013.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: The tenth resource widens the mode check, adds a nullable source column and four write-once, datastore-stamped tables holding no document or model file; the installer applies resources from a named ordinal and refuses a full install over a designated store.
- Scope of impact: M-012, M-013, M-014.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-005: Expected hashes written to the store by an installation command
- Benefits: Hashes travel with the store
- Risks: A store write before the go
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-009

2. O-004: Recordings read in place from the operator's folder
- Benefits: No copy of the recordings
- Risks: Bytes outside the writer's promote-once control between hash and decode
- Why not selected: Ties on hard constraints and impact; loses on reuse

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: The company store gains the own mode without a delivered row changing.
- Tradeoffs accepted: Delivered rows read their source from their mode.
- Risks introduced: R-008, R-009, R-014

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Every delivered row equal before and after application and re-application.
- Verification checkpoints: Application over a store of the company store's recorded shape in P-018.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A delivered row changed by the resource; the backup is then restored.
