# Architecture Decision Record D-007

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-007
- Title: Decoded-sample durations, one named loudness measure, an exact common-format join and the script's expectation
- Date: at emission in run-dfcae1756c32
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: architect (producer); omn-tech-lead (accepting owner of the Design Gate)
- Related Work Items: MC-11; the supplied execution plan's design tasks

## Context

- Problem statement: Every duration is read from a container field and the join re-encodes to 16-bit PCM.
- Business and technical constraints: C-003, C-004, C-008.
- Current architecture baseline: F-005, F-006, F-026.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-001
- Decision statement: One decode pass reports decoded samples, rate, channels, sample format and EBU R128 integrated loudness in LUFS; duration is samples over rate; the join keeps the common sample format and refuses a mixed set; the expectation reads the script's own rows, reported as a signed difference with no tolerance; the rendered runtime keeps the container basis, labelled.
- Scope of impact: M-002, M-004, M-005, M-011.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-006: Narration durations from the delivered container-field probe
- Benefits: No new measurement member
- Risks: Durations rest on a header
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-004

2. O-008: Mixed-format recordings resampled to the first file's format
- Benefits: No refusal of a mixed set
- Risks: A format target the owner did not set; joined audio differs from its parts
- Why not selected: Eliminated: violates C-008

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: Durations and loudness are measurements, never configuration.
- Tradeoffs accepted: A mixed-format recording set is refused.
- Risks introduced: R-004, R-006, R-017

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor: Joined sample count equal to the parts' sum within one sample.
- Verification checkpoints: A header-disagreeing fixture and tones of known level in P-007 and P-019.
- Rollback or reversal conditions: A recorded duration that differs from the decoded sample count over rate.
