# Memory: Decision Log

## Purpose
Persist significant engineering and product decisions with rationale, alternatives, and downstream impact.

## Structure

- Decision ID:
- Decision statement:
- Context:
- Options considered:
- Selected option and rationale:
- Impacted components:
- Risk and mitigation:
- Decision date:
- Review date:

## Ownership

- Primary Owner: Architect
- Approver: Tech Lead
- Contributors: Product Owner, Business Analyst, Reviewer

## Update Rules

- Log decisions when they alter architecture, delivery, or risk posture.
- Record rejected alternatives and tradeoff reasoning.
- Update status when decisions are superseded or rolled back.
- Include links to related workflows and artifacts.

## Consumers

- Architect and Tech Lead for design continuity.
- Orchestrator for dependency and risk routing.
- Implementers and Reviewers for implementation alignment.

## Lifecycle

- Proposed during analysis.
- Approved after decision gate sign-off.
- Superseded when replaced by a newer decision.
- Archived when no active components depend on the decision.
