# Skill Governance

## Purpose

Standardize how skills are authored, validated, and maintained across domains.

## Skill Contract

Every skill should include:

- Purpose
- Principles
- Best Practices
- Anti-patterns
- Decision Rules
- Common Mistakes

## Review Rules

- High-impact skill changes require architect or tech lead review.
- Security, testing, and observability skills require QA acknowledgement.
- Deprecated guidance must include replacement references.

## Maintenance Cadence

- Quarterly review for all skills.
- Immediate review after major stack or policy shifts.
