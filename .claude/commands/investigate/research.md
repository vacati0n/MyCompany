# Command: Research

## Purpose

Execute structured technical or product investigation via `workflows/investigate.md`.

## Required Inputs

- Decision to be informed.
- Key question and constraints.
- Deadline for recommendation.

## Assigned Agents

- Primary: `business-analyst`.
- Secondary: `context-agent`, `architect`.

## Outputs

- Investigation report with alternatives.
- Recommendation and risk rationale.
- Follow-up actions.
