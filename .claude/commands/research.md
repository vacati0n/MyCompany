# Command Specification: /research

## Purpose
Produce evidence-based recommendations for uncertain product or technical decisions.

## Inputs
- Research question.
- Decision deadline.
- Constraints and stakeholders.

## Workflow Triggered
Research (`workflows/research.md`).

## Expected Outputs
- Evidence log with confidence levels.
- Option analysis and tradeoffs.
- Recommended direction with implications.

## Success Criteria
- Recommendation is decision-ready.
- Risks and assumptions are explicit.
- Stakeholders can act on the output.

## Failure Handling
- Reframe scope when evidence is insufficient.
- Extend investigation for low-confidence findings.
- Escalate conflicting conclusions for arbitration.
