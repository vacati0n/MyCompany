## Metadata

- Feature name: MC-19 One-click button in the operator's own browser fills step 1 from the open Shopee product page
- Requested by: the CEO, by CEO decision 1318 of 2026-10-30 ("Mở MC-19"), after the two live reads of CEO decisions 1316 and 1317 (business intent, digest sha256:d0e0d5d777bc42f6a114377afff98eee; feature request, digest sha256:87ce06050698fc87d17cf66f7049a8ed)
- Business goal: The operator gets one product's facts and image choices into step 1 with one action on the product page already open in their own browser instead of copying by hand, with every fact still confirmed and checked, and with the company's exposure under Shopee's terms held to the bounds the CEO accepted.
- Target outcome: For one product per press, step 1 of the open video is filled from what the operator's own browser shows; the operator confirms each field and picks up to three images; the unchanged MC-16 check decides the save; the app sends no request to any shopee.vn page; with the read switch off the hand-over is refused and paste mode still works.
- Scope decision date: 2026-10-30

## Business Context

- Problem statement: At step 1 of the pilot web app the operator copies or pastes every fact of the product by hand, because the app's own read of a shopee.vn product page receives only a page shell with no product data (CEO decisions 1316 and 1317), while the operator's own browser shows every fact; the CEO's words: "làm manual rất take time" (doing it by hand takes a lot of time).
- Value hypothesis: Handing what the operator's own browser already shows to step 1 with one press removes most of the hand copying, while the per-field confirmation, the unchanged fact-sheet check and paste mode keep each fact's quality and a fallback, and the app itself still sends nothing to Shopee pages.
- Affected users: The operator of the pilot (the CEO now, an employee later), in their own browser and, when signed in, their own Shopee session (`Q-001`); the CEO as reviewer of each fact and approver of each video; the CEO's affiliate account, which carries the terms risk the CEO accepted in CEO decision 1313 item 5. Steps 2 to 7 and the MC-16 toolchain are affected only as named in `S-003` and `S-008`.
- Success measure: The inputs state the done condition only (`A-041`); this scope adds a count of operator actions per product (`A-005`, `D-009`); no business measure such as minutes per product is supplied, recorded as `Q-009`.
