# Business intent for MC-15 (the owner's decision, verbatim from research/ceo-decision-record.md)

## CEO-D-1300 — Pivot: affiliate revenue first, TikTok Shop and Shopee in Vietnam as the pilot

**The CEO's own decision, 2026-10-26**, after MC-14 (PR #13) merged.

1. The CEO judges that the video the company has been building (long narrated YouTube explainers, then 3D animation
   drawn by code) **cannot earn money** on the path the plan set: advertising revenue waits on partner-programme
   thresholds the company has not started toward.
2. **The company focuses on affiliate revenue.** The pilot is **TikTok (TikTok Shop affiliate) and Shopee (Shopee
   Affiliate) in Vietnam**, built as a model before anything else is expanded.
3. **Research first**: the pilot is scoped by a research ticket (`MC-15`, `pilot-affiliate-vn/00-ticket-mc-15.md`,
   routed as `/research`) whose recommendation the CEO approves before any build, account or download.
4. **The visual form is left to the research** to propose with options (real product footage, graphics drawn by code,
   or a mix), weighed against each platform's originality and disclosure rules and against cost; the CEO chooses.
5. **Wave 12 (MC-13) and Wave 13 (MC-14) are frozen, not cancelled.** The MC-13 feature branch stays as committed and
   unmerged; MC-14 stays on `main` as a deferred ticket. Their pipeline (deterministic rendering drawn by code,
   provenance, owner approval by hash) is available to the pilot if the research recommends it.

**What this reopens, and does not yet replace** (the research must say which it supersedes, for the CEO to rule):
`D-005` (advertising + affiliate; affiliate becomes the lead line), `D-007` (YouTube only at launch), `D-008` (one
English YouTube channel first), `D-022` (the first item carries no affiliate link) and the Vietnamese disclosure gap
carried by `D-021`. **Unchanged:** `D-002` (the CEO approves every publication individually), `D-018` (the CEO operates
as an individual creator and carries the legal position), the monthly budget and its metered ceiling, and that agents
never create platform accounts or handle the CEO's credentials.

Decision block: `CEO-D-1300`–`CEO-D-1399`, `CEO-C-1300`–`CEO-C-1399`.
