## Implementation Summary

- Change intent: A route's stated reasoning tier is now route data: the route register holds it in two shapes only, the composed registry passes it explicitly on every row, every shape reads back as written, and an operation recorded through the fully composed resolution path over a non-provider route stating Light for a request asking Deep reads back from the store with Deep requested and Light served, at zero units and zero cost. The tier ratio is the agreement share over records carrying both tiers and carries its definition as a required label. Waiting, claimed, retried, escalated and completed work for a caller-supplied half-open period is read from the append-only record, and an observed reading, an observed zero included, is now final by construction: every audit entry is stamped by the datastore at or above a record horizon that a datastore check enforces for every writer, a read raises the horizon only when no audited transaction is in flight, and a period reads observed only when it ends at or before the horizon its read set, so no entry can commit stamped inside a period already read; a read that finds transitions in flight on every bounded attempt reads unmeasured. Claimability is decided on one clock: every queue writer states a delay and the datastore sets the availability instant. The item dossier, the stage outcomes, the supply audits and the determinations each answer from recorded state through a recorded home, a transactional writer, a recorder service and a read port. One deterministic entry point drives a publishing unit through every declared position and comes to rest at composition or at the refusal the composer names, closing every stage row it opened and writing one completion entry, whatever the offset between the process clock and the datastore's. Each approval-effort component resolves from its own marks. Every datastore demonstration now drops its store when it completes as well as when it starts. Nothing publishes, nothing is spent and no precondition is recorded as satisfied outside a throwaway store that the demonstration itself drops.
- Approach taken: The selected option of the approved design was built as accepted and then amended as its finality decision record directs, with the departures recorded below. The fifth schema resource, the dossier writer and its closed payload-component union, the two read ports with their boundary summary records, the recorder, the entry point, the lifecycle action declaration, the per-component approval result, the five added read models and the one-row record horizon with its datastore check sit at rung seven of the necessity and reuse ladder, because no delivered component, standard-library type, platform capability or installed dependency holds a stated tier on a route row, a dossier home or writer, a named-unit claim, a throughput reading, a per-component approval result or a closed horizon for the append-only record; each was searched for first. The horizon reuses the datastore's shared, exclusive and non-waiting row holds that the delivered queue claim already relies on, at rung four. The payload serialization uses the standard library's JSON serializer at rung three, so no package was added. The named-unit claim, the terminal-stage closure, the per-component results, the datastore stamp and the delay form are each written at the one delivered site that already held the neighbouring rule. The drop on completion is one shared helper that the five datastore suites call from their existing lifecycle, at rung six. Every added analytics type is declared in the two scanned namespaces, every added string member is named on the authored-label list, and the entry point and the recorder sit in the rule-determined assembly, which gained no reference of any kind.
- Design reference: The approved technical design of this run under its selected option as amended after the review cycle, its decision records for the stated tier, the throughput reading, the four record sources, the publishing entry point and the finality of period readings, its inline decisions on the tier ratio, approval effort, held work, the deferred rate measures and the composition demonstration, its thirteen sequencing constraints, the ten build tasks the approved execution plan assigns to this phase, review package RP-2026-0003 with its four correction requests, and the Scope, Planning and Design Gate rulings and the tech-lead rulings on the review findings restated in the dispatch briefings, which are treated here as assumptions because a gate rationale reaches no downstream phase.
- Out of scope: No upload, channel creation, account creation, purchase, subscription session or render was added, the five structural absences are unchanged and none became a configuration value, and no sixth path exists. No first-publication condition, approval, gate transition or route is recorded by any production code, configuration, seed or migration; the only satisfied condition rows exist as fixtures inside stores the demonstrations drop. No provider account, endpoint or credential was created, no metered operation was run and the authorised exception stays unspent. No sizing figure, buffer depth, concurrency figure, target rate or pass line is stated anywhere, cycle time, failure rate and rework rate are declared deferred and computed nowhere, and no clip count or approval threshold was produced. No stage handler was built; the recorder has no production caller, which is the design's position. Resumption of an interrupted drive, the gate service's presentation from Draft and the absence of any writer of the awaiting-rights-check state were left as found. By the tech lead's rulings two conditions are carried rather than fixed: the month-period readings over the operation record have no finality rule, and the delivered audit chain can fork when two appenders read the same head; both are recorded as residual risks. The acceptance verification was not performed here, because this agent does not assess its own change. The numeric values of the assumed reasoning-tier split appear nowhere.

## Boundary Compliance

- Module boundaries preserved: The rule-determined assembly gained no project or package reference, its first-party references are still exactly the domain and the ports, and its closure still excludes the capability boundary, the credential broker and the persistence adapters; the entry point and the recorder sit in it, and the added assertion fails the build if either leaves it. Every added analytics type is declared in the two scanned namespaces, and no added port or adapter carries an analytics type, so the permitted-carrier list is unchanged. The horizon, the stamp and the delay form live below the ports, in the adapters and the schema; the composing site decides every case from the summary the reader returns.
- Public interface changes: The transaction interface gained the dossier writer and the job writer the named claim, both implemented by the datastore adapters and the three in-build doubles with no default body. The job writer's enqueue, release and advance now take a delay until claimability and return the instant the datastore set, and every caller moved in the same change. The dispatch service gained an overload taking the terminal-stage closure, the delivered signature delegating to it unchanged. The lifecycle gained the named claim and a delayed enqueue, and its enqueue returns the entered instant. The throughput summary carries the horizon and whether the read was quiet. The tier-ratio read model requires a definition, the analytics service takes four further read ports, and the approval measurement gained three component results. The internal audit appender takes no clock; the public unit-of-work constructor is unchanged. The route type, the capability boundary, the gate transition table, the composer, the dispatch outcome union, the workflow definition, the action set, the credential broker, the gate service and the publish-ready predicate are unchanged.
- Data or migration impact: One additive schema resource is applied after the four delivered ones, amended in place as the finality record directs because no kept store has applied it, creating each object only where absent: one nullable default-free checked column on the route register, five write-once tables with their checks and triggers, a partial unique index for the four singleton components, the one-row forward-only record horizon initialised while the append-only record is held against writers at the later of the datastore's clock and the latest stamp, a check refusing any audit entry stamped below it, and an index by action and instant. No delivered table, column, constraint, view or trigger is altered. Audit entries are now stamped from the datastore's clock, and queue availability is set from it. Reversal is lossless in the state delivered and becomes restore-to-point once a row exists. The demonstrations drop the schema of the database the test connection string names when they start and when they complete, and the record-store guide now points them at a database separate from the development schema.
- Declared side effects: The thirty-eight change-set paths, plus runs/run-423e990b743d/states/implementation/artifacts/implementation-report.md and runs/run-423e990b743d/states/implementation/result-envelope.json. Files edited temporarily for the reintroduced defects and seeded violations were restored from the committed head; of those, only src/MediaCompany.Deterministic/MediaCompany.Deterministic.csproj carries no change-set row, because its content is unchanged.
