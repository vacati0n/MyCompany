## Metadata

- Feature name: The sustained-rate capability, and the measurement that would make the rate decidable
- Requested by: the CEO and sole owner, through the supplied feature request `tasks/MC-6/input.md`
- Business goal: the company can decide on what it has observed, rather than on what it assumed, whether it is able to sustain its committed publishing rate, and can take that decision as soon as the owner discharges what only the owner can discharge
- Target outcome: every quantity that decision would rest on is readable from the company's own records and states plainly whether it was observed, observed as zero, or never measured; and the reasoning-tier split that the per-item cost and the monthly envelope rest on becomes testable by a production series rather than by a single demonstration
- Scope decision date: 2026-09-27

## Business Context

- Problem statement: the company has committed to a publishing rate it has never run, and the per-item cost and the monthly envelope that rate is priced against rest on a reasoning-tier split nothing has measured across three closed waves; meanwhile several of the readings the owner would need in order to judge whether the rate is sustainable either cannot be reached from the running system at all, or collapse an absence and a measured zero into the same appearance, so there is today no honest answer to the question the rate poses.
- Value hypothesis: a rate the company can decide on observation is worth more than a rate it can only assume, because every figure that rests on the assumption is a figure the owner cannot yet act on; making each reading say what it is worth costs nothing and removes the single largest unexamined premise under the company's economics.
- Affected users: the CEO and sole owner, who decides whether to sustain the rate, approves every publication individually and carries the cost of both; the operator roles that plan, price and run production against figures currently derived from an untested assumption; and the later change that first publishes, which inherits whichever readings this one did and did not make reachable.
- Success measure: every throughput, queue, effort and served-tier quantity the company publishes for a period is readable from its own records and resolves to exactly one of observed, observed zero, or unmeasured; no quantity that was never observed appears as a number anywhere; and an operation recorded by the fully composed path carries the reasoning tier the admitting route stated, with the absence marker appearing only where the route states none.

## Scope Decisions

Every decision that moved the boundary, with the rationale that justifies it.

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Impact | Decided By |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `D-001` | The change is bounded to the capability and the instrumentation, and the sustained-rate proof is excluded outright | The supplied request states the proof needs produced and published items, that producing and publishing are both spend or owner action, and that none of it is discharged; recording an obligation the change's own constraints foreclose would record an outcome nobody could accept at any gate | The change closes with the rate testable rather than tested, and the decision to sustain still waits on the owner and on a series that does not yet exist | omn-product-owner, on the supplied request |
| `D-002` | The authorised single-metered-operation exception is not drawn on, and no part of the boundary depends on it | The outcome this change needs is a stated tier reaching a recorded operation over the composed path, which an admitted non-provider route can carry at no cost; and one metered operation would not settle the split in any case, because the split needs a production series | The exception stays unspent and available to the owner, and no in-scope item becomes contingent on spend being authorised | omn-product-owner, with any spend decision reserved to the requesting owner |
| `D-003` | The four record sources are bounded as an outcome — their readings become answerable from the running system — and the route by which they become so is left undecided here | The supplied request records the matter at medium severity as belonging to the architecture role, and states that a deferral by a coordinating role is not an acceptance of the risk by the role that owns it; naming the mechanism here would decide what that role owns | The reachability is accepted as a deliverable at this gate, while where the records live and how they are reached is settled at solution design | omn-product-owner, with the technical route reserved to architect |
| `D-004` | The end-to-end production entry point is admitted, on the condition that the terminal position of the declared sequence stays terminal | The publishing wave carried the missing entry point forward as an open gap and, in the same record, carried a standing watch that the path must acquire no position after composition, because either addition would breach an absence the whole no-publish property rests on; admitting the caller under that condition delivers the carried item without weakening the absence | The sequence becomes exercisable end to end while the upload path stays unable to fire, and the condition is bound by a criterion rather than left as an intention | omn-product-owner, on the supplied constraint |
| `D-005` | No quantity is added outside the closed three-case measurement union, including every new throughput, queue and retry quantity | A zero meaning none occurred and a zero meaning never measured are different values; the union's unmeasured case carries no value field at all, so a null-coalescing default is not expressible, and the supplied request requires that discipline extended rather than weakened | Every quantity this change adds takes one of the three cases or the build refuses it by name, so the wrong rendering stays unexpressible rather than merely discouraged | omn-product-owner, on the supplied constraint |
| `D-006` | No required buffer depth, concurrency figure or sustainable-rate claim is recorded as an acceptance threshold, and the question is raised rather than absorbed | Any such figure would have to come from a cycle time, a failure rate or a rework rate, none of which has been observed and none of which may be invented; a figure with nothing behind it would be an assumption wearing a threshold's clothes, which is the failure this programme has spent three waves avoiding | The sizing half of the request leaves this phase unanswered and blocking, and the verdict is lowered accordingly rather than the boundary being quietly narrowed to fit | omn-product-owner, with the acceptance standard reserved to the requesting owner |
