## Objective

- Desired outcome: The capability a sustained publishing rate would need is present and instrumented on the company's own records, so that the rate becomes decidable on observation once the owner discharges what only the owner can discharge, while nothing publishes, nothing is spent, no precondition is discharged and no quantity that was never observed can be rendered as a number.
- Architectural objectives:
  - A route's stated reasoning tier is route data held by the route register and carried by the composed registry into the admitted route, in two shapes only, stated or stating none, with no default and no derivation from the requested tier expressible anywhere on the path. Traces to S-001, S-008.
  - The tier ratio a served-tier output presents is one definition, computed from the recorded requested and served pair alone, carried with the figure as a construction invariant, and never presented over fewer than two records that carry both tiers. Traces to S-001, S-008.
  - Waiting, claimed, retried, escalated and completed work for a period is read from an append-only record that the delivered lifecycle writes in the same transaction as each transition, and each quantity resolves to exactly one case of the closed three-case union at a single composing site. Traces to S-002, S-012, S-015.
  - A period reads observed, observed zero included, only when no entry stamped inside it can still commit, which a check in the datastore enforces for every writer, and a unit's availability and its claim are decided on the datastore's clock alone. Traces to S-002, S-004.
  - The item dossier, the production-path stage outcomes, the supply audits and the compliance determinations each have a recorded home, a transactional writer and a read port wired by the composition root, and the three quantity-bearing sources encode their measurement case in the row shape a table check admits. Traces to S-003, S-013.
  - One deterministic entry point drives a publishing unit through every position the delivered publishing workflow declares, in the order the definition declares them, and comes to rest at the terminal position or at a refusal the composer names, adding no position, no case, no action and no transport. Traces to S-004, S-006, S-007, S-010.
  - Each approval-effort component is derived from its own recorded marks at the single delivered derivation site, so a missing mark makes only its own component unmeasured. Traces to S-005, S-009.
  - Every type and authored label this change adds sits inside the scope of the build-time boundary assertions that run on every solution build, and every deterministic type sits in the assembly whose reference closure excludes the capability boundary. Traces to S-011, S-012.
  - Every reading carries a mandatory authored statement of what its record set does and does not establish, so no count read from any store can be presented as a sizing figure, and groupings rest on recorded identifiers. Traces to S-014, S-015.
- In scope:
  - The stated-tier column on the route register, its registry read, and what every route already recorded reads as.
  - The tier ratio definition and its presentability rule.
  - The throughput and queue quantities for a period, their source, their cases and their coverage rule.
  - The finality rule that makes an observed period permanent: store-assigned stamps, the record horizon and the check that refuses an entry stamped below it; and store-clock availability for the queue.
  - The recorded homes, the writer, the read port and the readings of the four record sources.
  - The publishing entry point, the named-unit claim it needs, and the terminal-stage closure the delivered dispatch transaction lacks.
  - The per-component resolution of approval effort.
  - The boundary assertions over every added type and label, and the demonstration contract the verification tasks run under.
- Out of scope:
  - Proof of the sustained rate, and any sizing figure: required buffer depth, concurrency figure, target rate, sustainable-rate assertion or pass line.
  - Cycle time, failure rate and rework rate as computed quantities; they are declared deferred and computed nowhere by this change.
  - Publication of any kind, and any upload, channel creation, account creation, purchase, authenticated subscription session or render.
  - Discharging any coded precondition, or making one satisfiable from configuration, seed data or a migration.
  - Any stage-handler implementation of the production path; the recorder this change adds is the writer such a handler would write through, and no handler is built.
  - A read port for approval effort over the recorded approvals; the delivered gate ledger already reads the approval marks, and no statement requires the reading reachable from the composed system.
  - Any clip count, any approval threshold, and any measured value for the assumed reasoning-tier split.
  - Any spend, any provider account, endpoint or credential, and any drawing of the authorised exception.
  - Any change to the gate transition table, the dispatch outcome union, the closed action set, the credential broker or the publishing workflow definition.
  - Resumption of an interrupted drive of the entry point on the same unit, which is raised as an open decision.

### 5.3 Selected Approach

- Selected: O-002, additive homes with typed tables for the quantity-bearing sources, throughput read from the append-only record, and the entry point as a deterministic service composing delivered steps.
- Structural change:
  - Stated tier (D-001). One nullable column is added to the route register, checked against the closed three-value tier set and carrying no default. A route row stating a tier holds it; a row stating none holds nothing, which is the same explicit absence the operation record's served tier already uses (F-004), so the encoding is one rule end to end. M-002 reads the column and passes it to the route constructor explicitly on every row, never relying on the constructor's default (F-002), and maps an absent value to the domain's absent tier and never to a default tier. Every route already recorded reads as stating none, which is exactly what the composed path read it as before this change (F-001), so no route acquires a tier it never stated. The capability boundary is unchanged (M-025): it already writes the stated tier on all five outcome paths that admit a route, including the three non-provider ones and the two failure paths before a provider is reached, and the absence marker on the two that admit none (F-003), so a non-provider route carries a stated tier exactly as a provider route does and the demonstration needs no provider route, credential or spend.
  - Tier ratio (D-002). The ratio presented is the agreement share: of the operations in the period whose record carries both a requested and a served tier, the share in percent whose served tier equals its requested tier. It needs no assumed split as an input and no ordering of tiers. It is unmeasured, with the reason that no observation exists and the count found as detail, whenever fewer than two records in the period carry both tiers, which also guarantees at least two served-tier records. The delivered meaning is kept and its denominator corrected: a record carrying a served tier and no requested tier no longer counts as a mismatch (F-005). The definition travels with the figure as a required authored label on the ratio read model, beside the delivered caveat and split-assumption label, so no output presents the ratio without saying what it is.
  - Throughput (D-003, D-007, D-008). Five quantities are read for a caller-supplied half-open period of instants (A-005): waiting, the units waiting to be claimed at the period's close; claimed, the claims made in the period; retried, the stage attempts released for retry; escalated, the units escalated to the dead claim state; and completed, the units reaching the terminal claim state. Each is counted from the append-only record by action and instant (F-019), because the lifecycle writes an entry for every transition in the transaction that makes it (F-016) whereas the queue table is overwritten (F-018). Waiting at the close is the arrivals into the ready queue up to the close — enqueues, non-final successes and retries — less the claims up to the close. The action names are declared once in M-013 and read by both the writers and the reader, so a renamed action cannot silently zero a count. The coverage rule decides the case at one composing site in M-010: a period lying wholly after the earliest entry of the record and ending no later than the record horizon the read itself established under D-010 is observed, a count of none there is an observed zero, and any other period is unmeasured with the reason that no observation exists and the coverage bounds as detail (A-006); a reconstructed waiting count below zero is unmeasured with the reason that the source cannot state one. The rule first emitted here decided elapse against the datastore's read instant while entries were stamped by the process clock before commit (F-038), which let a period read observed and gain entries afterwards; D-010 replaces that clause. The quantity the scope calls held is named waiting in the reading, because the delivered catalogue already declares a held-work measure sourced from resolutions held at the capability boundary (F-033), which is a different fact; a stage outcome recorded as held is not published, because no delivered step writes one (F-018) and a count of it would present a structural zero as an observed one. Cycle time, failure rate and rework rate are declared in M-018 as deferred measures carrying no value field and naming the production series each waits on, and are computed nowhere, because the only store in which they are computable now is a demonstration store whose every count is a demonstration parameter (A-001). The throughput read model carries a required authored statement that the counts establish what the store read recorded over the period and nothing about a sustainable rate, a required depth or a concurrency figure.
  - Four record sources (D-004). A dossier header row per item version is the observation boundary: it is written once when the dossier is opened. Stage evidence, supply-audit entries and determination resolutions each have a typed, write-once table keyed to the header, with closed-value checks and a table check mirroring the domain rule so that a row the domain type refuses cannot be written: a supply-audit row holds its count exactly as the entry holds it, a zero as zero and an absent count as absent, a present count carrying no unobtained reason, and a prior observation all present or all absent (F-022); a determination row carries the fields its outcome requires and no others (F-023). The remaining components — treatment verdicts, the audience designation, visual provenance, clip-origin assessments, claim attributions, metadata, the originality assessment and runtime — are held one row per recorded component in a write-once evidence table carrying a closed component kind and one structured payload, with the four singleton kinds unique per item version (A-007). One dossier writer is reachable only from the unit-of-work transaction (M-003, M-005, M-007), so each component and its audit entry commit together, as every delivered writer does. M-017 is the recording service a production step writes through; no production step exists to call it today (F-020), so in the company's own store every source reads unmeasured until one records into it, which is the empty-store behaviour the criteria require. One read port, M-004's item dossier reader, answers each source by its own member — the reconstituted dossier, its stage evidence, its supply-audit entries and its determination resolutions — returning absence where no header exists. M-011 composes each reading: every per-source count is unmeasured naming the register and the item version where no header exists, an observed zero where a header exists and no row, and an observed value otherwise; the stage-outcome reading runs the delivered predicate over the reconstituted dossier with the current gate state, the channel's registrations and the script-commit instant taken from the recorded script stage (F-024), and where no dossier exists it reports every stage unmeasured and evaluates no predicate rather than listing refusals over nothing.
  - Entry point (D-005, D-009). M-016 drives one item version with the value slots the delivered steps already take as parameters — declared subject, destination, treatment conditions, surface draft, settings and timing policy — and reads no control from configuration. It enqueues a unit of the delivered publishing workflow at the definition's first position, and for each position the definition declares, in the order its successor lookup returns them, claims that named unit (M-006, M-014, F-017), runs the position's delivered step, and records the outcome through the lifecycle, which advances the unit or brings it to rest. Surface production and timing are the delivered pure steps. At gate evaluation it evaluates the delivered gate over recorded state and presents the verdict, surfaces, settings, destination and planned instant to the delivered composer once (F-015): a refusal is persisted through the delivered dispatch service as a refused attempt naming the composer's reason and the gate's detail, which names every unsatisfied condition with unknown and absent visible (F-011), and the gate-evaluation stage is recorded under the delivered refusal class, which escalates the unit to the dead claim state; a composed descriptor advances the unit to composition. At composition it persists the descriptor through the dispatch service with the unit, no gate transition, and the unit's terminal-stage closure, and M-015 writes that closure and the lifecycle completion entry in the same transaction as the dispatch record and the terminal claim state, closing the pending stage row the delivered path leaves (F-014); a dispatch already recorded for the version brings the second unit to rest escalated, naming the existing record, with no second record written. The unit therefore reaches the composition position only with a passing verdict, and on the company's real recorded state, where no condition is satisfied (F-013), every drive comes to rest refused at gate evaluation. The entry point never presents, records, grants or shortcuts an owner approval, adds no position and declares no workflow definition, holds no reference to the capability boundary or the credential broker (F-031), and its type name carries none of the fragments the transport assertion refuses (F-030).
  - Finality of period readings and of availability (D-010). One row, the record horizon, holds the instant below which the append-only record is closed. Every audit entry is stamped by the datastore, not the process, as the later of the datastore's clock and the horizon, read while the appender holds the horizon shared; the shared hold lasts until the appender's transaction ends (F-041), and the stamp is taken before the entry's hash is computed, so the chain rule is unchanged (F-039). A check in the datastore on the append-only record takes the same shared hold and refuses any entry stamped below the horizon, so the property binds every writer, including one that bypasses the appender. The throughput reader obtains the horizon exclusively by a non-waiting attempt, which succeeds only when no audited transaction is in flight; it raises the horizon to the later of the datastore's clock and its prior value, takes every count in statements issued after the hold is granted, and the period is observed only if it ends at or before that horizon. No entry stamped inside a period read as observed can therefore commit after the read, whatever the offset between the clocks and whatever step the datastore's clock takes afterwards: an observed zero is permanent by construction. If the attempt fails on every one of a bounded number of tries, each quantity reads unmeasured because the source cannot state one, naming that audited transitions were in flight (A-009). The same one-clock rule is applied to the queue: every writer that makes a unit claimable — enqueue, release for retry, advance — states a delay until claimability, zero or the backoff, and the datastore sets the availability instant from its own clock plus that delay; no port member accepts an availability instant (F-040). The claim and the lease already use the datastore's clock, so no offset between the clocks can leave an immediately available unit unclaimable at the next position. The finality rule concerns period quantities only; the item dossier's counts are current-state readings of an accumulating record, and their required statement says they are as recorded at the read, claiming no permanence.
  - Approval effort (D-006). M-012 gains three per-component results computed independently in the delivered derivation: review from the presentation and decision instants, queue from the queued mark and the presentation, rework from the rework link, the predecessor's decision and the presentation, each either a derived interval or a statement naming the mark that is absent or inconsistent. The delivered whole-row resolution, the three nullable intervals and the missing-quantity statement keep their exact meaning, so the approval surface exercise and the datastore view read unchanged (F-025). M-010 maps each component from its own result: a derived zero is an observed zero, a derived non-zero interval an observed value in minutes, an absent mark unmeasured because no observation exists, and inconsistent marks unmeasured because the source cannot state one, each naming the mark. The review component's marks are mandatory by construction and in the table (F-026), so its missing-mark case is unrepresentable rather than unmeasured. No threshold, target or pass line is stated on any component.
  - Boundary assertions (P-010). Every added read model is declared in the domain analytics namespace and every added composer in the rule-determined analytics namespace, so the delivered membership and member rules scan them; every added string member is an authored label named on the delivered list; no added port or adapter carries an analytics type, so the carrier list is unchanged; one assertion is added placing M-016 and M-017 in the rule-determined assembly, so the no-model closure provably covers the entry point and the recorder. The nested-type exemption and the one-level member rule are carried as limits (F-008), and no added analytics type is nested.
- Rationale: O-002 is one of three options that satisfy every hard constraint, and the three tie on impact surface at three contract changes and no dependency change. It leads O-005 on reuse leverage, because it counts throughput from the append-only record the lifecycle already writes in every transition's own transaction rather than adding a record every transition path must remember to write. It ties O-004 on reuse, quality attributes and migration burden, and leads it on operability: the components normalized only by O-004 carry no measurement quantity and are read by the publish-ready predicate whole, so seven further tables would add install, backup and reversal surface with no reader that queries a column. The selection therefore follows from the evaluation table on the third and sixth criteria.
- Highest-scoring rejected alternative and why it lost: O-004 satisfies every hard constraint and ties O-002 on impact surface, reuse leverage, quality attributes and migration burden; it loses on operability alone, adding seven tables beyond O-002's five for components that no reading queries by column and whose field rules the domain types already enforce at construction.
- Tradeoffs accepted:
  - The seven non-quantity dossier components rely on a payload round trip rather than on per-field table checks; recorded as R-018 and mitigated by P-006.
  - The waiting quantity is reconstructed from the append-only record and is correct only while every claim-state change goes through the delivered services; recorded as R-004.
  - A drive is not resumable on the same unit, so an interrupted drive leaves a unit resting mid-sequence, visible in the waiting and claimed readings; recorded as R-016 and raised as Q-007.
  - The delivered dispatch transaction gains two writes, the terminal-stage closure and the completion entry, to make the completion count and the stage history exact; recorded as R-009.
  - The boundary summary records the new ports return carry bare counts outside the scanned namespaces, exactly as the delivered cost summaries do, and are turned into quantities only at the composing site; recorded as R-002.
  - Under D-010 every audited transaction takes a shared hold on one row until it ends, a throughput read writes that one row and briefly holds it exclusively, and a read that never finds a quiet instant returns unmeasured rather than waiting; audit stamps come from the datastore's clock rather than the process clock; recorded as R-021, R-022 and R-023.

## Operational Considerations

- Logging and observability updates:
  - M-017 appends one audit entry per recorded dossier component in the component's own transaction, so every recorded row is answerable for who recorded it and when; the entry point adds no audit action of its own and relies on the delivered lifecycle, gate and dispatch entries (F-014, F-016).
  - M-015 appends the lifecycle completion entry when it brings a unit to its terminal claim state, so the completion count reads one action on every path (D-003).
  - Every unmeasured reading names what was looked for and where: the register and item version for a source, the coverage bounds for a throughput period, and the mark for an approval component.
- Error handling strategy:
  - Absence is a value, not an error. A source with no header, a period outside coverage and a missing mark each resolve to the unmeasured case with a closed reason; nothing is filled in (C-004, C-016).
  - A refusal at gate evaluation is a completed outcome, not a failure of the entry point: it is persisted as a refused attempt, recorded as the stage's failure reason, and the unit rests escalated in the dead claim state under the delivered refusal class, which never retries (F-016).
  - A recording into a dossier that was never opened, a second singleton component, or a row the table check refuses fails the transaction, leaving neither the row nor its audit entry (C-005).
  - A reconstructed waiting count below zero means the record is incomplete and resolves to unmeasured rather than to a number (A-003).
  - A throughput read whose non-waiting attempt at the record horizon fails on every bounded try resolves every period quantity to unmeasured because the source cannot state one, naming that audited transitions were in flight; an entry stamped below the horizon is refused by the datastore and its whole transaction with it, which the appender cannot produce because it stamps at or above the horizon (D-010).
- Security considerations:
  - Security impact was assessed for every added record and reading. The added tables hold evidence text authored by production steps and hold no credential, secret or identity column; the dossier reading presents counts and authored labels only; the delivered read models that already surface record-derived text are unchanged; and the throughput reader selects only the action and instant columns of the append-only record, never its reason, subject or reference fields (C-013).
  - No endpoint, account, credential or external dependency is added. The entry point and the recorder sit in the assembly whose closure excludes the credential broker and the capability boundary (F-031), and the release credential remains refused while any condition is unmet (F-010), so the absence that holds a hypothetical transport without anything to present is untouched (C-001, C-003).
  - Owner approval is read, never written, by the entry point: it presents nothing, records no verdict and passes no gate transition, and the demonstration records the verdict only through the delivered gate service (C-008).
- Performance considerations:
  - No performance constraint or quality attribute was supplied for this change, and no latency, volume or rate figure is asserted. The one access-pattern consideration is structural: the throughput reading is the first reader of the append-only record by action and instant, which the delivered index by subject and instant does not serve, so the fifth resource adds that index (M-001). Under D-010 every audited transaction holds one row shared until it ends, which does not serialize appenders against each other, and a throughput read holds it exclusively only for its own count statements after a non-waiting grant, so it never queues behind an appender and no appender waits on it for longer than those statements; no latency figure is asserted. No sizing figure is derived from any measurement of the reading itself (C-010).
- Deployment and operability impact:
  - A schema install applies five ordered resources rather than four, the fifth additive (M-001). The composition root gains registrations for the two adapters, the recorder and the entry point, and the check command resolves them without a datastore (M-019). No process, endpoint or configuration key is added, and the provider endpoint list stays empty (C-003).

- Rollout: the fifth schema resource is appended and applied at install; the port members and every in-build implementer land together; the action names are shared before the throughput reader counts them; the recorder and the reader land before the source readings; the named-unit claim and the dispatch closure land before the entry point; the boundary assertions are extended last among the build steps and run on a solution build; demonstrations run only against stores they drop.
