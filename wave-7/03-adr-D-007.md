# Architecture Decision Record D-007

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-007
- Title: Caller-less currency members and the budget reader are retired, and the period summary and served-tier list carry their month closure from the same read
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-8; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0008; planner tasks T-008, T-020 and T-033; sequencing constraints P-003, P-007 and P-011

## Context

- Problem statement: the served-tier list and the currency-returning cost members carry no month finality, so a caller reads a month with no statement of whether it can still change.
- Business and technical constraints: C-004 requires a figure and its finality from one read; C-003 keeps the horizon unweakened; C-016 requires every delivered caller's change to be named.
- Current architecture baseline: F-021 and F-022 record the close-then-read transaction and the finality rule; F-023 lists the members; F-024 records their production callers; F-025 records the delivered retirement condition; F-026 records the budget reader's place in the factory.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the item cost, period cost, cost by capability class, deterministic-set cost, period headroom and three-state period cost members and the budget reader are removed under the delivered retirement condition, none having a production caller; the period summary and the served-tier list are read inside the close-then-read transaction and return the closure with their figures; the report service's period cost, served tiers and tier ratio take finality from that read; the horizon rule is unchanged.
- Scope of impact: M-003 removes and changes the members; M-008 implements them; M-022 consumes them; M-027 re-points the checks.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-006, keeping every member and giving each a closure-carrying return.
- Benefits: no member is removed.
- Risks: seven members are kept for checks alone.
- Why not selected: it ties on impact surface and loses on reuse leverage, because it does not reuse the delivered retirement condition.

2. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-007, O-008, O-009, O-010 and O-011, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: no reachable month reading over the served-tier records or the cost members lacks finality.
- Tradeoffs accepted: delivered checks are re-pointed; a minor version carries removed members.
- Risks introduced: R-006, re-pointed checks; R-030, an outside binding.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of reachable month readings over those records without a finality statement; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of production callers of a retired member; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of readings presented as final that change on a later read; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Review of every month reading over the operation record.
  - A reading presented as final, unchanged after a later commit.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if an outside consumer of a retired member is found; the member returns with a closure-carrying signature as O-006 would.
