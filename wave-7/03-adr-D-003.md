# Architecture Decision Record D-003

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-003
- Title: Selection ranks on minimum observed quality and maximum observed cost only where every candidate is fully observed in one unit, read in one statement, and records its basis and inputs
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-8; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0008; planner tasks T-002, T-014 and T-027; sequencing constraints P-005, P-006 and P-013

## Context

- Problem statement: the route register orders routes by configured ratings a reader could take for evidence, and nothing yet selects on observed quality and cost or says which basis a selection rests on.
- Business and technical constraints: C-010 requires ranking only on fully observed quantities in one unit and the configured ordering otherwise; C-004 requires one read at one datastore instant; C-018 forbids an invented observation minimum or statistic parameter; C-015 places the rule in the rule-determined assembly.
- Current architecture baseline: F-009 records the delivered resolution steps and the route-tier ordering; F-013 records the route tiers and the floor scale; F-037 records the routing rule of the cheapest capability that reliably meets the floor; F-033 records the rule-determined assembly.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: among the survivors of the delivered steps one to four within the best eligible tier class, evidence applies only where every candidate has at least one observation for the request's task class, none unmeasured in quality or cost, every quality in the floor's rating unit and every cost in the ceiling's currency; each candidate's figures are its minimum observed quality and maximum observed cost; the selection is the cheapest candidate meeting the floor, ties by route tier then identifier, and a request whose candidates all fall below the floor on observation is held; otherwise the selection is the route-tier ordering exactly, labelled configured, naming each unmeasured quantity, unit mismatch or absent task class. The evidence is one statement on the admission transaction; the decision record carries the basis, every candidate and the observations ranked on with their count; the register is never written.
- Scope of impact: M-006 reads the evidence; M-013 applies it; M-015 ranks; M-017 adds the task class; M-018 declares the basis read model.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-007, keeping delivered resolution below the downgrade action.
- Benefits: no tier classes before ranking.
- Risks: a lower stated tier is ranked and served silently.
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-007.

2. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-008, O-009, O-010 and O-011, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: the first real observations change selection with no build; no configured rating is presented as observed; every evidence selection states its count.
- Tradeoffs accepted: worst-case figures are conservative; a single unmeasured observation returns the configured ordering.
- Risks introduced: R-004, rendering; R-016, the aggregation; R-021, requests without a task class.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of evidence-labelled selections resting on an unmeasured input; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of selections returning a route below the floor or matching a forbidden source; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of decision records whose inputs differ from what a re-read of the referenced observations returns; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Over fixture observations, each compared quantity of each candidate made unmeasured in turn yields the configured ordering exactly.
  - An observed-zero cost beside an unmeasured cost over otherwise identical candidates.
  - A concurrent observation commit during a ranking leaves one read's inputs recorded.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the owner sets another aggregation or a minimum; the one ranking site changes and the configured basis is kept.
