# Architecture Decision Record D-001

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-001
- Title: The capability boundary reserves one datastore booking instant at the start of one admission transaction held to commit, and reads prices, controller readings and evidence on it
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-8; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0008; planner tasks T-003, T-007, T-016, T-019, T-032 and T-037; sequencing constraints P-004, P-006 and P-013

## Context

- Problem statement: admission reads the headroom and its month at one datastore instant and the unit prices at the process clock's instant, the provider is called, and the operation is then booked at a later datastore instant, so a call spanning a month end is admitted against one month and booked into the next, prices are read on two clocks, and a held outcome's escalation instant is the process clock's.
- Business and technical constraints: C-009 requires admission against the booked month; C-004 requires every instant to be the datastore's and every decision to read its inputs once; C-003 requires the record horizon extended, never weakened; C-019 keeps the chain head off any external call; C-005 keeps cost arithmetic in the datastore.
- Current architecture baseline: F-003 records the reads before resolution and their clocks; F-004 records the transaction opened after the call; F-005 records the recorder's stamp under the shared horizon hold; F-007 records the booking check; F-010 and F-011 record the zero-priced estimate and the process-clock instants; F-021 records that closures never wait; F-041 records the default isolation and the chain head.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the boundary opens its transaction before resolution; its first statement reserves the booking instant as the later of the datastore's clock and the record horizon under a shared hold on the horizon kept to commit, held in a transaction-local datastore setting so no caller code carries it; prices, controller readings and evidence are read on that transaction at that instant; resolution takes it as its instant, so escalation instants derive from it; the provider is called with the transaction open; the recorder books at the reserved instant; the decision record, the audit entry and the delivered budget evaluation for the reserved month follow, and the transaction commits. A transaction lost during a call is replaced by a fresh one under a fresh reservation whose decision record states the loss and re-evaluates against the booked month.
- Scope of impact: M-002 declares the admission member; M-005 books at the reserved instant; M-006 reserves and reads; M-011 restructures the boundary; M-012 drops the budget reader; M-013 takes the reserved instant.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-001, keeping the transaction after the call and re-reading the booked month there.
- Benefits: no transaction spans a provider call.
- Risks: the cost is incurred before the booked month's headroom is read.
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-009.

2. O-003, a durable reservation row with closures capped at the earliest open reservation.
- Benefits: no long transaction.
- Risks: an abandoned reservation holds every month open until an expiry nobody recorded.
- Why not selected: it ties on impact surface and loses on reuse leverage, because it replaces the transaction-scoped shared hold with a new table.

3. O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009, O-010 and O-011, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: every operation books into the month it was admitted against; prices are read and applied at one instant; every instant the boundary records or derives is the datastore's.
- Tradeoffs accepted: one connection and one shared horizon hold per call in flight; month readings and throughput read not final or unmeasured during a call; an operation books at its admission instant.
- Risks introduced: R-001, readings during a call; R-002, admission-instant booking; R-010, a retroactive price row; R-029, a lost transaction.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of operations whose booked month differs from the month their decision record was admitted against; the expected value is zero outside a stated lost reservation.
  - The count of recorded instants on the process clock; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of closures that waited; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - In a throwaway store, a stand-in provider raising the horizon past a month end during its call, with the operation, its decision and its alerts in the admitted month.
  - The same admission repeated with the process clock skewed a day behind and ahead, every instant equal to a datastore instant.
  - Review that the chain head is first taken after the call returns.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if holding a transaction across a provider call is rejected; the boundary returns to the delivered order and O-003 is designed with an expiry the owner records.
