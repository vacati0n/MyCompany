## Implementation Summary

- Change intent: Every capability admission now reserves one booking instant from the datastore before resolution and keeps it to the commit, so the prices, the cost controller's channel and company readings, the evidence, the operation, its decision record and its alerts are on one clock and in one month, across a month end included; a write-once benchmark record holds content-free corpus entries and observations whose quality is row-encoded in one of three cases and whose cost and latency are the referenced operation's own; selection ranks on evidence only where every compared quantity of every candidate is observed in one unit and otherwise returns the configured ordering labelled and naming the gap; a pure cost controller maps each reading to none, the alert only, a downgrade, a deferral or a refusal by the four fixed thresholds, applies the most severe, and refuses metered work under its own reason where no budget amount is recorded or spend cannot be stated, never reading either as zero or headroom, while zero-cost targets stay admissible; resolution serves a lower stated tier only as a recorded downgrade for a routine request; a missing price is removed rather than estimated at zero and every operation states whether its cost is stated; recorded amounts are their own two-shape type; caller-less cost members and the budget reader are retired and the remaining month readings carry their own closure; a tier distribution reading exists; gate transitions carry a mandatory position, are write-once and name a same-instant outcome; the production and publishing keys are admitted under a company scope; every assembly carries version 1.4.0; and, after the correction cycle, one provider attempt is booked exactly once under one identifier minted before the call whatever fails after it, a cancellation after the call never rolls the record back, metered admissions are serialised per scope with the company scope held before the channel scope, the company's metered allotment caps each estimate, every delivered cost, utilisation and alert reading honours whether each cost is stated, the admission rests on one snapshot whose prices are the ones booked, the company ceiling reading states the controller's basis, and a company scope must name a registered company
