# Architecture Decision Record D-010

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-010
- Title: The production and publishing keys are admitted by the configuration store under a company scope only, every delivered fragment rule unchanged
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-8; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0008; planner tasks T-010, T-022 and T-035; sequencing constraints P-009 and P-013

## Context

- Problem statement: the configuration store admits only the six base keys and the channel key set, so the production and publishing settings cannot be recorded through it, and the decision on how to admit them was routed to the role that owns no phase.
- Business and technical constraints: C-012 forbids weakening any fragment rule or admitting a key that reaches approval, the payee, a precondition or an upload absence; C-006 keeps every key away from the controller; C-002 keeps the payee company-level.
- Current architecture baseline: F-027 records the pairing rule and that no production code reads these keys; F-028 records the three key sets and their rules, and that every publishing key name contains a fragment the channel rule forbids.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: a company scope form names exactly one company register identifier; a production or publishing key is admitted only under such a scope and only where its own delivered rule finds no control; a base key keeps its delivered pairing and a channel key its own; no fragment rule changes; the controller reads none of them.
- Scope of impact: M-023 declares the scope form; M-008 applies the pairing rule; M-038 is unchanged.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-010, admitting them under a channel scope.
- Benefits: per-channel production values.
- Risks: the channel rule would have to admit names it forbids.
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-012.

2. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009 and O-011, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: every production and publishing setting records and reads back under one scope, with no control reachable.
- Tradeoffs accepted: per-channel variation stays in the channel key set.
- Risks introduced: R-013, a channel-scoped write; R-033, an owner preference for per-channel settings.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of production or publishing keys recorded under any scope but a company one; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of fragment rules changed; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of keys read by the controller; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Each key round trips under a company scope and is refused under a channel scope, in a throwaway store.
  - Review of the delivered key sets against every rule.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the owner directs per-channel settings; a decision on the channel fragment rule precedes any move.

- Product Owner (if scope-impacting): omn-product-owner for the scope question; unsigned
