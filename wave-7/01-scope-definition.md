## Metadata

- Feature name: The AI-economics capability: benchmark record, evidence-ready model selection and deterministic cost controller, with no metered benchmark run
- Requested by: the CEO and sole owner, through the supplied feature request `tasks/MC-8/input.md`, read together with the owner's decision of 2026-10-09 against a metered benchmark in this wave, recorded at the end of the repository's CEO decision record in the feature worktree
- Business goal: the owner can trust that every model choice and every spending action the company takes says truthfully whether it rests on evidence or on a configured ordering, and that spending against the monthly envelope is held back by fixed rules before it overruns rather than noticed afterwards
- Target outcome: model selection ranks on observed quality and cost when observations exist and visibly falls back to the recorded configured ordering when they do not; spending actions at the four utilisation thresholds follow fixed rules with defined behaviour where a budget reading is unmeasured; the assumed reasoning-tier split becomes decidable once a production series exists; and the five items carried from the previous wave are closed
- Scope decision date: 2026-10-09

## Business Context

- Problem statement: the company has spent nothing on model calls in six waves, so it holds no observation of any model's quality, cost or latency, yet the route register orders routes by configured ratings that a reader could mistake for evidence; utilisation is tracked at the four thresholds but nothing acts on a crossing before the month's envelope is exhausted, and a channel with no recorded budget amount currently reads as zero headroom and is refused as if its budget were exceeded; the cost model rests on a reasoning-tier split that nothing the company records can confirm or refute; and five defects or limits carried from the previous wave remain open.
- Value hypothesis: if every quantity a selection or a spending action rests on is labelled observed, observed zero or unmeasured, and the rules that act on them are fixed and deterministic, then the first real observations can change model selection with no further build, the envelope cannot be overrun silently, and no decision the owner takes rests on a configured number presented as a measurement.
- Affected users: the CEO and sole owner, who holds the monthly envelope of USD 77.41, decides every spend and reads every cost figure; the shared operator and agent roles whose requests the router serves and the controller governs; and the later change that first records a real observation or a real production series, which inherits whichever readings this one makes decidable.
- Success measure: over a freshly installed store every benchmark quantity reads unmeasured and every selection reads as the configured ordering, labelled so; over fixture observations in a self-dropping demonstration store the same selection ranks on evidence and labels it so; each of the four thresholds and the unmeasured budget reading yields exactly its defined action with zero model calls; and each of the five carried items reads closed in the change account.
