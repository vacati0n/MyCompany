# Architecture Decision Record D-004

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-004
- Title: The cost controller is a pure fixed rule over the channel and company readings of the booking month, with booked spend read from the complete ledger, the recorded metered allotment as interim company basis, its own refusal reasons, zero-cost routes admissible and unpriced routes refused
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-8; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0008; planner tasks T-003, T-005, T-016, T-017, T-028 and T-030; sequencing constraints P-005, P-006 and P-013

## Context

- Problem statement: utilisation is tracked at four thresholds but nothing acts before the envelope is passed; a channel with no budget reads zero headroom and is refused as exceeded; a scope with no operation reads its cost unmeasured, which would refuse the first metered operation of every month; a missing price is estimated at zero cost; and the company reading divides metered cost by an envelope that includes a standing charge the record does not carry.
- Business and technical constraints: C-006 fixes the mapping and keeps configuration away from it; C-008 forbids reading an unmeasured reading as zero or headroom; C-013 keeps controls uncut; C-014 keeps metered spend within what the envelope leaves after the standing charge; C-015 places the rule in the rule-determined assembly; C-018 forbids an invented figure.
- Current architecture baseline: F-007 records the booking check binding every writer; F-008 records the zero headroom where no budget exists; F-010 records the zero-priced estimate; F-014 records the zero-cost targets; F-015 records the threshold set and alerts; F-016 records the envelope constants; F-033 records the rule-determined assembly.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: the governing readings are the channel reading and the company reading of the booking month, read in one statement at the reserved instant; booked spend reads observed, observed zero where nothing is booked, and unmeasured naming the operations where a booked operation's cost is unstated; the channel reading's amount is the recorded channel budget or not recorded; the company basis is the recorded metered allotment, stated on every output, until the owner answers otherwise; each reading maps to none below 50, alert only at 50, downgrade at 75, defer at 90 and refuse at 100, a missing amount and unmeasured spend each refusing under their own reason; the most severe action applies; under refuse, defer or an unmeasured reading only hold-and-escalate and substitute targets stay admissible; a provider route lacking a price for any metered unit kind is removed under its own reason; the recorder states whether each cost is stated; the controller takes no configuration dependency and reaches no control.
- Scope of impact: M-005 records cost-stated; M-006 reads the readings; M-013 restricts targets and removes unpriced routes; M-014 decides the action; M-016 names it; M-017 declares the reasons.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-008, governing the company reading against the full envelope.
- Benefits: matches the delivered company reading and the plan's interim assumption.
- Risks: metered spend can reach the envelope while the standing charge pushes the total past it.
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-014.

2. O-001, O-003, O-004, O-005, O-006, O-007, O-009, O-010 and O-011, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: no metered work proceeds against an amount nobody recorded or a spend nobody can state; the first operation of a month is admissible; the refusal says why.
- Tradeoffs accepted: the company basis refuses earlier than the full envelope would; booked spend reads observed zero while the per-channel cost reading reads unmeasured; an unstated cost refuses the rest of the month for its scope.
- Risks introduced: R-009, concurrent admissions; R-011, an unstated cost; R-015, the company basis; R-020, the two zero readings; R-031, spend outside the system; R-032, a dropped deferral.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of decisions whose action differs from the mapping of their most severe reading; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of refusals for a missing amount or unmeasured spend stating that a budget was exceeded; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of configuration keys the controller reads; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - A fixture channel placed in each band, two channels against the company basis, a channel with no amount and a scope with an unstated cost.
  - A hold and a substitute admitted at 100 percent and under an unmeasured reading.
  - The first metered operation of an empty month admitted.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed in part if the owner answers the company basis or the observed-zero boundary otherwise; one constant or one reading rule changes.
