# Architecture Decision Record D-002

## Metadata

- ADR ID: D-002
- Title: The benchmark record is a content-free entry register and write-once observations with row-encoded quality, and cost and latency taken from the operation each observation references
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Proposed
- Owners: omn-tech-lead as the accepting owner of the Design Gate under the producer exclusion rule; architect as the producing agent
- Related Work Items: MC-8; technical design package DESIGN-2026-0008; planner tasks T-001, T-013 and T-026; sequencing constraints P-001, P-003, P-004 and P-013

## Context

- Problem statement: no record holds any observation of a model's quality, cost or latency, the corpus is empty and may not be authored, and the record must admit exactly three cases per quantity from every writer while its instants stay the datastore's.
- Business and technical constraints: C-003 keeps the union closed and every case row-encoded; C-005 keeps every cost the datastore's arithmetic; C-004 keeps instants on the datastore's clock; C-001 and C-018 forbid recording or inventing any value in the company's store.
- Current architecture baseline: F-019 records the closed union; F-020 records the row-encoded discipline; F-006 records the generated cost; F-021 records the horizon; F-033 records the rule-determined boundary; F-037 records the six representative tasks.

## Decision

- Selected option: O-002.
- Decision statement: a corpus-entry register holds an entry identifier, its task class and its datastore registration instant and no content; an observation references its entry and task class together, references one operation together with its route and model, carries its quality as an observed value with a non-zero amount and a unit, an observed zero with a unit and no amount, or unmeasured with a closed reason and a detail and nothing else, and is stamped by the datastore under the shared horizon hold, booked into its month, refused below the horizon or before its operation, and write-once; cost and latency are read from the referenced operation in their three cases; the task class set is closed at the six representative tasks and refines the capability class, so a request names it and the comparison key is the pair.
- Scope of impact: M-001 creates the tables and checks; M-002 declares the writer; M-003 declares the reader; M-007 implements both; M-017 declares the task class; M-018 declares the readings.

## Alternatives Considered

1. O-004, writer-supplied cost and latency columns.
- Benefits: an observation stands without an operation row.
- Risks: a benchmark cost disagrees with the operation record.
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-005.

2. O-005, a fourth union case.
- Benefits: none for this record.
- Risks: every renderer gains a case.
- Why not selected: eliminated by C-003.

3. O-001, O-003, O-006, O-007, O-008, O-009, O-010 and O-011, the other options of the package evaluation.
- Benefits: each carries this decision unchanged.
- Risks: each differs from O-002 in another design area only.
- Why not selected: none is decided on this record.

## Consequences

- Positive outcomes expected: no writer can commit another shape; a benchmark cost is the operation's own; a fresh store reads every quantity unmeasured naming what was looked for.
- Tradeoffs accepted: an observation needs a recorded operation; a request must name its task class to receive evidence.
- Risks introduced: R-003, a bypassing writer; R-004, rendering; R-021, requests without a task class.

## Validation Plan

- Metrics to monitor:
  - The count of observations outside the three shapes; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of observations stamped below the horizon or before their operation; the expected value is zero.
  - The count of observations in the company's store; the expected value is zero.
- Verification checkpoints:
  - Each permitted shape round trips and each forbidden shape written directly is refused, in a throwaway store.
  - An observed zero and an unmeasured quality rendered side by side.
  - The boundary assertion that no record path references the capability assembly.
- Rollback or reversal conditions:
  - Reversed if the owner directs that benchmark cost come from outside the operation record; the record is dropped while empty and redesigned.
